What does “research use only” mean?

ZUMA compounds are sold for laboratory research. They are not for human or veterinary use, and they are not drugs, foods or cosmetics. We can’t advise on use in people or animals. At checkout you confirm research use and that you are 21 or older.

How is each lot tested?

Every lot is tested for identity by mass spectrometry and for purity by HPLC. Its report is published before the lot ships, and the code on each vial opens that lot’s report. At launch, each report’s fingerprint is also recorded on-chain.

When will my order ship?

In-stock orders ship within one business day, cold-packed, with tracking emailed when they leave. Orders over $250 ship free. A compound whose next lot is still in testing ships the day its report is out.

How should vials be stored?

Store unopened vials at −20 °C, away from light. The storage conditions for each lot are on its report.

How do subscriptions work?

Choose Subscribe on any compound and it ships every 30 days at 10% off. Skip, pause or cancel anytime before the next order is placed.

How can I pay?

Card checkout is open to everyone. At launch you can also pay with USDC, SOL or $ZUMA. $ZUMA is a utility for the ZUMA shop and not an investment.

Returns and damaged orders

Vials are temperature-sensitive research materials, so shipped vials can’t be returned. If an order arrives damaged or wrong, write to us within 7 days with your order number and we will replace it.

What do the colors mean?

Each compound wears the three tones of its research area: metabolic blue, tissue green, dermal red-orange, endocrine amber. A research area names the field a compound is studied in.
