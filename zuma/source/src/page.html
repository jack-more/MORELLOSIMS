The rules, in short

  • 12 months, locked in

    A customer stays linked to you for 12 months from their first paid order. A later link or code can't move them.

  • 30-day clearing

    Each reward clears after 30 days at the lower of your level when the order was paid and your level when it clears. Refunds and chargebacks reverse it.

  • 7-day holding

    Your level uses each wallet's lowest daily balance over the last 7 days, so buying the day before an order won't lift it.

  • One level only

    You earn on the customers you refer, never on the customers they refer.

  • No self-referrals

    Your own orders never count, including orders from your email, wallet, payment method or shipping address.

Sharing rules. Every referral post must include “For laboratory research use only. Not for human or veterinary use.” and must never suggest use in people. Paid referrers disclose the relationship. Rewards and levels are a loyalty program and not an investment.

About

Built for where performance and longevity research is going.

ZUMA started with a small team that wanted research compounds it could trust without asking twice.

So we kept it simple. Six research peptides, each in the colors of its research area. Small lots, filled fresh. Every lot is tested by HPLC and mass spectrometry, and its report is published before it ships, with a code on every vial that opens it.

We are building for the next decade of performance and longevity research in the lab: faster lots, open reports and records anyone can check.
