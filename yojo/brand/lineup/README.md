# Spin-off lineup

## The call

Make one GHK-Cu hero. Everything else complements it.

- A second GHK-Cu bottle would compete with the hero and blur the story. Keep copper in the egg.
- Each spin-off does a job the cream does not: cleanse, hydrate, calm, lips. Together they make a routine, and a routine sells as a set.
- Every piece shares the fixed core:
  - a satin-chrome knurled part
  - soft-touch chalk-color body
  - molded tone-on-tone type, no ink
  - YOJO over one ingredient or product word
- The shape changes with the job. This is the Fortnite logic: the core is the same and the skins change.
- A second GHK-Cu product, such as an eye cream, can come later, once the hero has proved itself.

## The routine

| Step | Product | Shape (from your references) | Color | Why it pairs with GHK-Cu |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Cleanse | Cleansing bar on a rope | Frosted pebble with a Y inside (soap-on-rope ref) | Frost | Gentle, low-strip cleanse that sets up the cream |
| 2 Hydrate | Hyaluronic serum | Dropper with a knurled chrome collar (Outside-In ref) | Chalk Sky | Water-binding layer under the cream |
| 3 Treat | **YOJO GHK-CU cream** | The egg | Chalk Sky | The hero |
| 4 Light layer | YOJO JELLY water-gel | Stacking jars with knurled chrome lids (stacked-jars ref) | Chalk Rose | Daytime or oily-skin moisture on top of or instead of the cream |
| 5 Calm or lips | YOJO POP: cica gel tube *or* lip stick | Molded-type tube (green tube ref) or twist stick (Laneige ref) | Sage / Cobalt | Calming, barrier-friendly; neither competes with copper |

**Launch set:** the bar, the serum and the cream. They are the cheapest to tool:
- The serum uses a stock dropper bottle with a custom chrome collar.
- The bar needs almost no packaging.

JELLY and POP then arrive as season drops.

**Bar idea:** make the Y inside the bar a silicone charm. The bar wears down until the Y is freed, and the customer keeps it. You unlock it by using the product.

## What stays out of the line

- **Vitamin C (L-ascorbic acid) and strong exfoliating acids (AHA/BHA):** they can destabilize the copper peptide. If customers use them, the guidance is vitamin C in the morning and copper at night.
- **SPF:** in the US, sunscreen is an OTC drug under FDA rules, with drug testing and registration. It would be a later product made with a specialist partner.
- **Formulator note:** ask Xiran and the others to keep strong chelators such as EDTA out of the GHK-Cu formula, because they can bind the copper.
- **Claims:** cosmetic only. See `../../BRAND.md`.
