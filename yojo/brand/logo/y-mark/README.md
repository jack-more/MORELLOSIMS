# Y mark

This is the Y cut from your own YOJO wordmark (the vector trace in `../yojo-wordmark-*.svg`), so the mark and the wordmark match.

- `yojo-y-ink.svg` and `yojo-y-white.svg`: the mark on its own.
- `yojo-y-head-icon.svg`: the Y on the chrome head disc. Use it for the app icon, favicon and social avatar.
- `y-mark-studies.png`: the Y on each palette color, tone on tone, the head icon, and the Y | YOJO lockup.

Two rules:
- Never put a circle or ring around the Y. A Y inside a circle reads as a peace sign. The round head is enough of a frame.
- Have a type designer clean up the edges before it goes on a mold or into print. The trace has small wobbles.
