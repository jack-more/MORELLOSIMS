### Vetting rules we set
- Ask for **% of pure GHK-Cu** in the finished product, not % of a "peptide complex"
- Base must have **no vitamin C (ascorbic acid), AHA/BHA or EDTA**; pH about 5–7
- Ask for the **ISO 22716 / GMPC certificate** and the **FDA facility registration number** (there is no "FDA certificate" for cosmetics)
- Get the raw-material **COA**, then send a finished sample to a US lab for **HPLC** potency testing
- Start with private label, 300–500 units, samples from 2–3 factories

## 5. US regulation

- MoCRA overview (FDA): https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-laws-regulations/modernization-cosmetics-regulation-act-2022-mocra
- Facility registration and product listing (Cosmetics Direct): https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/registration-listing-cosmetic-product-facilities-and-products
- Cosmetic vs drug claims: https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/laws-regulations/it-cosmetic-drug-or-both-or-it-soap
- Labeling claims hub: https://www.fda.gov/labeling-claims
- Acne products are OTC drugs, Monograph M006: https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/omuf/Order/Final%20Administrative%20Order%20OTC000013_M006-Topical%20Acne%20drug%20products%20for%20OTC%20Human%20Use.pdf
- ISO 22716 (cosmetics GMP standard): https://www.iso.org/standard/36437.html

Rules: you are the "responsible person" (product listing, safety records, adverse-event reports, US name and address on label). Keep claims cosmetic ("visibly firmer-looking skin"), never "boosts collagen", "repairs" or "treats acne". Sunscreen and acne products are OTC drugs, so skip them for launch.
