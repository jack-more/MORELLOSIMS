# YOJO content brief: video and image, season 1

Draft for review · 2026-11-03

Nothing is shot until this is signed off. Every series name below is a working label for this document, not public copy.

---

## 1. What the content has to do

**The position.** GHK-Cu is everywhere, and almost nobody uses it properly. YOJO is GHK-Cu done right, inside the routine that actually works.

**What "done right" means.** These are true, specific and showable:
1. **Copper hates air and light.** YOJO is airless. The cream never sees air until it leaves the head.
2. **Copper reacts with metal.** Nothing metal touches the cream on its way out.
3. **Copper gets bound up by the wrong ingredients.** There are no chelators like EDTA in the formula, and the pH sits at 5 to 7, where GHK-Cu stays stable.
4. **Copper fights vitamin C and strong acids.** The routine keeps them apart: vitamin C in the morning, copper at night, acids on other nights.
5. **The routine order:** cleanse (AMINO), hydrate (HYALURONIC), treat (GHK-CU), protect (SPF in the morning).

**The job of each piece.** Every piece does one of three things:
- **Desire:** make people want to hold the object.
- **Understanding:** make people get why copper needs this.
- **Ownership:** make people want to own, collect and show the skins.

## 2. Guardrails, every piece, no exceptions

**Never show:**
- cutaways, internals, the thread or pump mechanism in section
- dimensions, the factory or supplier names
- the chip, Locker or serial plan before we announce it
- formulas we haven't announced. The routine can be shown as steps; the other three products appear only once we've decided to reveal them.

**Claims are cosmetic only.**
- **OK:** "visibly smoother", "firmer-looking", "softens the look of fine lines", "hydrates".
- **Never:** "boosts collagen", "repairs", "heals", "anti-inflammatory", "treats acne".
- Everyone on camera, creators included, gets this list in writing before filming.

**The product is always right:**
- The head sits level; the head is never tilted.
- The Y on top is correct, and so is the wordmark on the front: YOJO over GHK-CU, debossed, never printed.
- Colors are the calibrated skins: Ice, Bubblegum, Matcha, Oat, Cobalt.
- **No AI-generated product in motion.** The AI tests proved it: the product warps and looks pasted on. The product in motion is either our 3D model or a real physical prototype.

**Copy:** no taglines until YOJO writes or approves them. Supers carry facts only: GHK-CU · 30 ML · AIRLESS · TWIST TO OPEN.

## 3. How we make it

| Layer | Made with | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Product on its own (hero, twist, press, carton, skins) | Our 3D model, rendered in Blender | It's the real object, exact every time, with calibrated color |
| Product in hands and on faces | A live shoot with a physical prototype | Real hands and skin can't be faked convincingly |
| Physical prototype | Resin print of the shell and head, painted soft-touch, chrome-plated or PVD head, real silicone skin cast from a printed mold | Two or three hero units are enough for a shoot. Needed before any people content. |
| Science visuals | 3D and macro photography of real cream (blue, because copper peptide is blue) | It's specific and true |

**Order:**
1. Model-only content can start now.
2. People content waits for the prototype, about 2 to 3 weeks for a print-and-paint unit.

## 4. The series

### A · The Twist (signature, product only, 3D)
**What it is.** Six- to ten-second loops of the head turning: knurl under the fingertip, the head rising on chrome threads, a soft detent click, a press, a ribbon of blue cream out of the side slot. One episode per skin color.

**Why it works.** This is the gesture nobody else owns. It's satisfying, loopable and instantly recognizable.

**Shot list per episode:**
1. Locked, top-down: the Y.
2. Three-quarter: the twist up, the threads rising.
3. Macro: the knurl turning, shallow depth of field.
4. The press, with cream out of the slot.
5. The twist down, locked flush. Hold.

**Sound** (designed, no music on the master):
- knurl friction (a fine metallic rasp)
- one click per third of a turn
- the pump's soft exhale
- a felt thud when it locks flush

**Look:** black void with cool rim light, or tone-on-tone on its own skin color.

**Formats:** 9:16 master, 4:5 and 1:1 cut-downs, and a silent version for in-feed.

### B · Why It's Blue (understanding, 3D plus macro)
**What it is.** Fifteen- to thirty-second explainers that make the chemistry beautiful. One idea per episode:
1. GHK-Cu is a copper peptide, which is why the cream is blue.
2. Air and light break it down: hence airless.
3. Metal reacts with it: hence the metal-free path.
4. Vitamin C cancels it: hence the routine.

**Visual language:**
- a slow drop of blue cream in macro
- the egg turning on black
- clean, minimal supers with the fact in mono type

There's no presenter in the first round, just voice-over or supers.

**Rule:** every claim is a formulation fact or a cosmetic claim. No biology promises.

### C · The Order (understanding plus routine, live)
**What it is.** "What routine actually works," shown on a real face. Morning and night, in order, with the egg in shot.

**Cast:**
- One cosmetic chemist or dermatologist as the voice of authority. They're paid and briefed on the claims rules.
- Two or three faces across skin types and ages.

**Structure:**
- Myth: "Copper and vitamin C together."
- Truth: "Separate them: C in the morning, copper at night."
- Show: the routine done right.

**Needs:** the physical prototype.

### D · Skins (ownership, fashion editorial, live plus 3D)
**What it is.** Each skin color dropped like a fashion release: an editorial still set, one six-second motion piece and a 3D turntable.

**Styling:**
- Wardrobe in tonal family with the skin, for example Bubblegum on a dusty-rose knit set.
- Hair and makeup glowy and minimal. The product is the jewelry.

**Sets:** tone-on-tone paper sweeps matched to each skin, plus one black-void hero per drop.

**Drop rhythm:** a teaser (the skin's color only), the reveal, then the carton.

### E · The Carton (ownership, 3D plus live)
**What it is.** The six-egg tin opening: the lid lifting, six colors, the sound of brushed aluminum. It's the collector moment and the gifting moment.

**Shots:**
- the top-down reveal
- a slow push across the six heads
- one egg lifted out by a hand (live)

### F · The Wobble (reach, playful, UGC-ready)
**What it is.** The egg is weighted, so you tip it and it rocks back upright. Short, funny and physical.

**Use:** creator prompts and the hook for paid social.

**Needs:** a working prototype to prove it really self-rights. Test before scripting.


### G · The Grove (the world, animated, flagship series)
**The lore.** YOJO doesn't come from a factory in this world. The pods grow on trees.
- Soft, pastel, egg-shaped pods hang in a floating orchard, blank and unfinished.
- A pod only becomes YOJO when someone plucks it and drives the chrome nail (the knurled head) into it.
- Applying it keeps you looking fresh, which is what lets you stay in the game season after season.

**Why it's ours:**
- It turns the product's real gesture (head in, twist, press) into the core game action.
- It turns skins into seasonal drops that fit a battle-pass structure.
- It gives every future product a place in the world: new trees, new pods, new seasons.

**Beats** (the 30–45 s pilot):
1. **The Grove:** a wide shot of the orchard at golden hour. Pods ripen and glow from inside.
2. **Pluck:** an original hero character climbs and picks a ripe Ice pod.
3. **The nail:** a low hero angle. They drive the chrome head into the pod with a burst of blue light, and the blank pod becomes the finished vessel with YOJO over GHK-CU pressed in.
4. **Twist and press:** the real gesture, one turn up, cream onto a fingertip.
5. **Apply:** a dab on the cheekbone and a soft glow, like equipping a new skin.
6. **The season tease:** another tree in the distance ripens in a new color. This is the next drop.

**Characters.** Original only: our own cast in a stylized battle-royale look. No Fortnite characters, likenesses, weapons, emotes or logos; that's Epic's intellectual property. One way in later, if you want it: a licensed Fortnite collaboration, as a business deal with Epic.
- Cast three or four heroes with distinct silhouettes.
- Each hero "mains" one skin color, so the characters double as the colorway story.

**Make:**
- **The pilot:** a stylized 3D animation studio. Our product model drops straight in as the hero asset, so the vessel stays exact.
- **The world look:** AI concept frames for lookdev only, because the product must come from the model.
- **Cutdowns:** 6 s character loops for social, plus stills of each hero holding their pod.

**Claims.** Keep "stay young" in the lore as game language, like staying in the game. On screen and in captions it's cosmetic only: fresh-looking, glowing, smoother-looking skin. Never "reverse aging" or "younger skin" as a product promise.

**What it unlocks:**
- seasonal drops told as harvests
- collectible character and skin pairs
- a reason to come back every season

## 6. Look and feel

- **Light:** black void, one strip light from above, cool rims. Or tone-on-tone daylight on paper.
- **Color:** the calibrated skins only. Chrome is the only shiny thing in the frame.
- **Camera:** slow, deliberate moves. Macro for the gesture, three-quarter for the object.
- **Type on screen:**
  - the YOJO wordmark
  - one display face (the brand pick, still to confirm)
  - IBM Plex Mono for facts in caps
  - no more than six words on screen at a time
- **Pace:** the product never rushes. One idea per cut, with holds of a second or more on the locked state.
- **Sound:** designed foley over music. Music only on brand films, and it's licensed.

## 7. Casting and talent

- **Faces:** three to four, varied skin tones, ages roughly 22 to 45. Real skin texture with no heavy retouching; the claim is skin that looks better, so it has to look real.
- **Hand model:** one, with natural short nails in a neutral or sheer finish. The hands do the twist, so cast for them.
- **Expert:** one cosmetic chemist or dermatologist. Don't put "Dr." in any brand name or handle.
- **Releases:** every person, usage term of 2 years minimum, all paid platforms.
