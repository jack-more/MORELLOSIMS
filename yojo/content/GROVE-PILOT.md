# The Grove: pilot commercial

Draft for review · 2026-11-05 · 30–40 s · 16:9 master, 9:16 cut

Series G in the content brief. The lore: pods grow on trees, and a pod becomes YOJO when someone plucks it and drives the chrome nail in. Applying it keeps you fresh enough to stay in the game, season after season.

## How it's made

| Layer | Made with |
|---|---|
| Characters, the orchard, the action | Stylized 3D animation from AI frames (Higgsfield: GPT Image for frames, Kling 3.0 Pro for motion) |
| Every shot where the product is the subject | Our Blender model: the real vessel, the snake-rope head, calibrated skins |
| The product in a character's hand | Wide or mid shots only, product small in frame. Any close-up of the product is a Blender insert. |

No Fortnite characters, likenesses, weapons, emotes, map or logos. The look is "stylized battle royale": chunky proportions, saturated color, clean shapes. The cast below is ours.

## Cast: the Fresh Squad

Each hero mains one skin color, so the cast doubles as the colorway story.

| Hero | Mains | Silhouette | Vibe |

## Claims

"Young forever" stays in the lore: it's the name of the game the heroes are playing, said by characters about staying in the game. Nothing on screen, in captions or in voice-over promises younger skin. Allowed on screen: fresh-looking, glowing, smoother-looking skin. Never: anti-aging, reverses aging, younger skin, collagen, repair.

No tagline until YOJO writes or approves one. The end card carries facts only.

## Production order

1. Lock the cast: 4 hero turnarounds, 3 frames each. About 12 frames.
2. Lock the world: the Grove wide, a ripening pod, the far tree. About 8 frames.
3. Storyboard frames for beats 1–8. About 16 frames.
4. Animate beats 1, 2, 3, 6, 7 and 8 from the frames. About 10 shots, 3 takes each.
5. Blender inserts for beats 3–5 and 9. These can start now; they need no credits.
6. Cut, sound design, 9:16 reframe.

Budget: about 10 credits for frames and about 225 for shots, about 250–300 in all.
