# Brand research: skincare, fragrance and collectible systems

Compiled 2026-10-27 for the YOJO brand book. Every fact comes from a page the researchers opened; "n/d" means no opened source documented it. Pages that blocked automated access are marked *[indexed]* (search text only).

---

## The short version

1. **The object is the brand, not the logo.** People remember Glossier's pouch, Aesop's amber glass, the Jean Paul Gaultier torso, the Daisy cap and Rhode's lip case before they remember the name.
2. **Lock the core, change the surface.** Diptyque's oval, Le Labo's label and Aesop's codes never change. Labels, caps, colors and boxes do.
3. **An accessory you carry beats a new product.** Rhode's silicone Lip Case (440k+ waitlist, $9M+ reported), Crocs Jibbitz ($270M+ in 2024) and Guerlain's swappable lipstick cases all make the shell the collectible.
4. **Scarcity needs a story and fair rules.** Ffern's solstice drops, Owala's Vault and Stanley's partner colors work. Fights, fakes and blind-box backlash are what happens without rules.
5. **A character or persona carries the content.** Starface's Big Yellow is the strongest example in skincare.
6. **Clinical facts make the playfulness credible.** The Ordinary's ingredient-plus-percentage naming, and Rhode's neutral bottles against food names.
7. **Retail and refills are rituals.** Le Labo prints your name and the fill date on every refill.

## Collectible and drop systems

| System | How it works | Results | Pitfalls | For YOJO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Game item shop** (Fortnite mechanics) | Rotating shop with leave dates; named series; rarity was color-coded (green / blue / purple / orange) | ~80% of recent players engaged with cosmetics (Naavik); players buy for uniqueness, status, identity (CHI PLAY 2020) | FTC $520M settlement over dark patterns and child privacy; removing tiers read as a price hike | Rarity at a glance, tied to material and finish. Publish leave dates. No dark patterns. Don't use Epic's names or terms. |
| **Pop Mart / Labubu** | Blind boxes: 6 regular + 1 secret at 1/72 | 2024 revenue ~$1.8B; Labubu ~$423M (+729%) | Store fights (UK sales paused), counterfeits, **China's draft rules ban cosmetics in blind boxes** | Never blind-box the cream. Mystery only for skins, with printed odds and a direct-buy option. |
| **Stanley** | Partner-exclusive colors, no restock | $73M (2019) to $750M+ projected (2023); ~700M TikTok views | Fights, resale, later quality backlash | Partner-exclusive skins, small batches. |
| **Owala** | Color Drop page: countdown, Notify Me, Sold Out, **The Vault** archive, playful names | Sell-outs in minutes (weak source) | | A public Vault page builds lore. |
| **Crocs Jibbitz** | $3–5 charms that fit the shoe | $270M+ in 2024; ~3 in 4 buyers add them, worth ~2x | | The vessel is a platform. Freeze the fit so skins stay compatible for years. |
| **Starface** | Character + refillable compact; mystery collab compacts | Hello Kitty collab sold out in 72 hours | | Name the vessel and give it a voice; only the shell is collectible. |
| **Guerlain Rouge G** | Pick a shade, pick a case, swap either | Designer cases (Kévin Germanier) | New refills don't fit old cases, which orphaned collectors | Closest beauty analogue. Never break compatibility. |

## What this means for the YOJO brand book

**Fixed core, never changes**
- The vessel silhouette and the chrome screw head. It should be recognizable from its outline alone.
- The wordmark.
- A spec plate on every unit: GHK-Cu and its percentage, in the same place every time.
- A consistent touch signature: the feel of the twist and the click when it locks.

**Variable surface, changes every season**
- Silicone skins, by color, finish and texture.
- Juice color, by formula.
- Box art, which can be by a different artist each season.
- Collab skins. These never change the formula or the silhouette.

**Systems to design in the book**
1. **Seasons:** a public calendar of four a year, each with a numbered run and a Vault archive of retired skins.
2. **Rarity:** a ladder YOJO owns, set by material and finish rather than borrowed game colors. The screw-head finish can signal the tier.
3. **Numbering:** season, drop and edition on every skin, plus the owner's name and fill date on refills.
4. **Character:** decide whether the vessel has a name and voice.
5. **Fair-drop rules:**
   - The formula is never rarity-gated.
   - Any mystery format is skins-only, with printed odds and a direct-buy option.
   - Online queues and per-customer limits.
   - Built-in authentication on limited skins.
6. **Retail and content:** sets built to be filmed, and refills as a ritual.

**Avoid**
- Millennial pink and "clean beige" minimalism.
- Throwaway extras in every box.
- A lab or apothecary look as the whole identity.
- Borrowed nostalgia.
- Any random sale of the cream itself.
