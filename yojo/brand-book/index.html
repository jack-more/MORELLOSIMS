YOJO BRAND BOOK · VOL.0102 NO DEBT

THE STARTING POINT

No debt.

Most beauty brands carry years of old bottles, old claims and old logos. Every new idea has to fit around what's already on the shelf.

We start with nothing to undo. One object. One active. Built right the first time.

No design debt. No product debt. So the product gets to be the whole brand.

YOJO BRAND BOOK · VOL.0104 WORDMARK

The O's are eggs. Both are the product's silhouette, filled solid.

The second egg wobbles. It sits at 12°, our wobble angle.

Y and J stay thin. Wide, square-ended strokes so the eggs carry the weight.

YOJO BRAND BOOK · VOL.0105 WORDMARK USE

Clear space: one egg width on every side.

Minimum size: 64 px wide on screen, 18 mm in print.

Colors: white, ink or sky only. Never on silver, never outlined, never stretched.

YOJO BRAND BOOK · VOL.0113 VOICE

HOW WE TALK

Short. Specific. A little smug.

Say what it is and what it does. Use real numbers and real ingredient names. Let the object be the joke; the copy stays dry.

Cosmetic claims only: visibly smoother, firmer-looking, hydrated. Never "boosts collagen", "repairs" or "treats".

Don't.

  • No outlined or stretched wordmark.
  • No lab benches or medical styling.
