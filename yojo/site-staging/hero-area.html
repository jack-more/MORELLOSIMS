OK, what is GHK-Cu?

A copper peptide: three amino acids holding on to a copper ion. It's one of the most talked-about anti-aging ingredients around, for skin that looks firmer and smoother with softer-looking fine lines.

How do I use it?

Twist the head up one turn, press once, smooth it over clean skin after your hydrating step. Twist it back down and you're done.

Can I use it with vitamin C or retinol?

Yes, just not at the same time. Vitamin C in the morning, YOJO at night. Acids and retinoids get their own nights.

How long does one egg last?

One press is about 0.2 ml, so a 30 ml egg gives you roughly 150 presses.

Do I toss the egg when it's empty?

Nope. The cream lives in a refill cup inside. Pop the old one out, drop a new one in, keep the egg.
