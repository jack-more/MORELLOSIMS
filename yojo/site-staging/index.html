How it works

Twist, press, lock.

One full turn raises the head on its thread. Press once for a dose from the spout under the Y. Turn it back down until it sits flush; locked, it stays shut in a bag.

Inside, a refill cup sits on an airless pump. When it runs out, the cup comes out and a new one goes in. The egg stays.
The copper rules

Built around copper.

Copper peptide is everywhere. Most of it is packed, mixed and used in ways that work against it. YOJO is airless, the whole cream path is plastic, the formula has no chelators and sits at pH 5 to 7, and the routine keeps vitamin C and acids apart from it.
The nail

Engraved by hand.

The head carries the official Y, raised and polished, in a field of fine turned rings. Round the side, a twisted rope weaves between two rope edges, with leaf sprays in every pocket. The spout sits in a polished cartouche under the stem of the Y.

The engraving is cut once by hand and cast, so every head is the same.
