Questions.

What is GHK-Cu?

GHK-Cu is a copper peptide: a small chain of three amino acids holding a copper ion. It's one of the most talked-about anti-aging ingredients in skincare, for firmer, smoother, younger-looking skin.

How do I use it?

Twist the head one turn to open, press once for a dose, and smooth it over clean skin after your hydrating step. Twist it back down to lock. Use it at night, or morning and night if you don't use vitamin C.

Can I use it with vitamin C or retinol?

Yes, just not at the same time. Keep vitamin C for the morning and GHK-Cu for the night. Give acids and retinoids their own nights.

How long does one egg last?

Each press is about 0.2 ml, so a 30 ml egg gives roughly 150 presses.

Is it refillable?

Yes. The cream sits in a refill cup inside the egg. When it runs out, the cup comes out and a new one goes in, and the egg stays.
