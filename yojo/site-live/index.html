The Egg

Copper is fragile.

Copper peptide is everywhere. Most of it is packed, mixed and used in ways that work against it. The egg is built around what copper needs.

  1. Air and lightJars and droppers expose it every time they open.Airless. The cream meets air only as it leaves the head.
  2. MetalCopper reacts with metal parts in ordinary pumps.Nothing metal touches the cream on its way out.
  3. The wrong formulaChelators and the wrong pH pull copper out of the peptide.No chelators. pH 5 to 7, where GHK-Cu holds together.
  4. The wrong routineVitamin C and strong acids layered on top cancel it.A routine that keeps them apart.
