# YOJO brand guide

Version 3 · 2026-11-13 · yojo.skin

Version 2 (2026-10-28) is retired: it described a plain knurled head, a crate, and no batch system. Everything below is what has been built and approved since.

## 1. The system in one line

One GHK-Cu hero in a weighted silicone egg, crowned by an engraved silver nail. Every other piece (skins, carton, stamp, site) exists to make that object feel collected, made by hand and true to copper.

## 2. The routine

| Step | Product | Status |

| 1 Cleanse | Gentle, pH-balanced cleanser | Not yet announced |
| 2 Hydrate | Hyaluronic serum | Not yet announced |
| 3 Treat | **GHK-Cu cream in the egg, the hero** | Launch |
| 4 Protect | Daily SPF (morning) | After launch (OTC drug) |

- In public, the routine is shown as steps only. The other formulas stay unnamed until YOJO announces them.
- **Customer guidance:** vitamin C in the morning, copper at night. Acids and retinoids on their own nights.

## 3. Product rules (from the GHK-Cu research)

- **Copper lives in one product only, the egg.**
- **No product in the line may contain:** L-ascorbic acid (vitamin C), AHA/BHA acids, retinoids, benzoyl peroxide.
- **All formulas:** finished pH 5–7.
- **The GHK-Cu cream:** peptide added last, below 40 °C; no EDTA or other chelators; no fruit acids or ionic plant extracts.
- **Nothing metal touches the cream.** The pump, its spring and the outlet are plastic, and the outlet runs in a PP liner right to the slot face, even when the head is silver.

## 4. The object

### The egg
- Body Ø65.6 × 77.5 mm, a little taller than round, tapering toward the head. Overall height 86.5 locked, 95.5 open.
- Soft-touch platinum silicone skin over a rigid shell with a steel weight, so it rocks back upright.
- YOJO over GHK-CU debossed on the front, tone on tone.

### The nail (the head)
- Ø36 × 9 mm. One turn up opens it, one press doses, one turn down locks it flush.
- **Top:** turned micro rings (0.36 pitch, 0.4 deep) around the official Y, raised and polished flat.
- **Side (Epic, Legendary):** the snake rope. A twisted rope weaves between two rope edges, with leaf sprays and bright-cut fans filling every pocket. All metal, with no blackened ground.
- **Common:** a clean diamond knurl.
- **One line, always:** the spout, the stem of the Y and the front wordmark sit on a single vertical line. The spout sits in a mirror-polished cartouche framed by a small twisted-rope oval.
- The exposed thread is solid satin silver, never mirror chrome, so it never reads as glass.
- **Make the engraving once, by hand:** a hand-engraved master, cast by lost wax. Never laser.

### Tiers

| Tier | Body | Head |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Silicone skin | Satin silver PVD, diamond knurl |
| Epic | Silicone skin | Brass cast from the master, silver plated, oxidised, relief polished |
| Legendary | Brushed sterling, wordmark smoked | 925 sterling cast from the master |

The formula is identical at every tier. Only the outside changes.

## 5. Logo

- **Wordmark:** YOJO (`site-live/assets/wordmark.svg`).
- **Y mark:** the Y cut from the wordmark. It lives on the head top.
- **No single outline ring around the Y** (it reads as a peace sign). The turned micro rings are a texture, and they are allowed.
- **The head always sits level**, and the Y stem always points to the spout.

## 7. The batch stamp

Real eggs are stamped at the farm. Ours are too.

- **Face: YOJO Batch.** A halftone serif. The dots grow and shrink with the letter shape, and YOJO is the real wordmark rebuilt in dots. Source: `model/stamp/natural.py`.
- **Printing: medium.** Pressure fades toward the edges, ink spreads a little, tone is mottled, and a rare satellite droplet appears. The dots always stay distinct and countable.
- **Layout:** every stamp is level and centred. YOJO over the batch code (B0001). No tilted or scattered stamps.
- **The odd egg:** one egg-white egg per grid carries the full batch block in copper ink: YOJO / GHK-CU / BATCH 0001 / date.
- **Format:** `BATCH 0001` and `YYYY.MM.DD`, the production date of the GHK-Cu batch. 0001 and the current date are placeholders until production gives real numbers.
- **Where it goes:** the front of the egg (the batch grid), the carton lid, the hang tag.

## 8. Packaging

- **The carton:** molded pulp, six cups, cone posts, a domed lid with a flat label strip, and a latch tab. Natural off-white, or dyed Chalk Sky.
- **The hang tag:** a white card with silver foil (YOJO, GHK-CU, copper peptide cream, 30 ml airless, twist to open), a sky card behind, a sky cord, and a strap with the Twist, Press and Lock icons.
- **The sign:** sky acrylic, four silver standoffs, YOJO / GHK-CU / TWIST / PRESS / LOCK in silver foil.

## 9. Photography and image

- **Catalog:** the product on a warm white or warm grey sweep, with soft daylight and a short shadow.
- **Tone on tone:** each skin on a backdrop in its own colour.
- **Batch grid:** a 4 × 4 grid of eggs, straight on, on batch green with fine film grain. One egg-white egg in copper.
- **Macros:** the micro rings, the snake rope, the twist up, cream leaving the spout.

## 10. Type

- **Wordmark:** YOJO only, never retyped.
- **Stamp:** YOJO Batch (section 7).
- **Site and supers:** Instrument Serif italic for names, Inter for everything else. This is in use on the site now. The final display face is still open.

## 11. Words

- Use only real product words on product: YOJO, GHK-CU, COPPER PEPTIDE CREAM, COPPER TRIPEPTIDE-1, 30 ML, AIRLESS, TWIST / PRESS / LOCK.
- No taglines or product names until YOJO writes or approves them. Descriptors like "Copper Care" are placeholders.
- Skip hype words: "miracle", "clinical-grade", "revolutionary".

## 12. Claims (US)

**Cosmetic claims only:** how skin looks and feels.
- **OK:** "visibly smoother", "firmer-looking", "softens the look of fine lines", "hydrates", "fresh-looking".
- **Not OK:** "boosts collagen", "repairs", "heals", "treats acne", "anti-inflammatory", "anti-aging", "younger skin".
- "Young forever" lives only in the Grove lore, as game language. It is never a product promise.
- SPF is an OTC drug (FDA Monograph M020). Any acne claim makes a product a drug too (M006).
- **Never use "Dr."** in names or copy.

## 13. Don'ts

- No Asian characters.
- No tilted or scattered stamps.
- No single ring around the Y.
- No mirror-chrome threads.
- No Fortnite, Chrome Hearts or other brands' characters, marks or ornaments. Take the craft (silversmithing, rope, bright-cut engraving), never the motifs.
- Don't show internals, specs, factories or unannounced formulas in marketing.
