# ZUMA sticker set · Post Spanish (direction A)

Seven die-cut stickers, 70 × 100 mm art + a 2.2 mm white border (74.4 × 104.4 mm overall): one per research area (Metabolic · RT-3, Dermal · GHK-Cu, Endocrine · Tesamorelin, Tissue · BPC+TB, Neuro · Selank, Lab supplies · BAC water) and one family sticker with all six Lightboxes.

- `print/zuma-sticker-*.png`: transparent PNG, about 500 dpi, white border built in. Upload as-is to a die-cut printer (Sticker Mule, StickerApp), "die cut", no extra border. Matte vinyl.
- Every sticker: Oxblood logo on the clean stucco wall, the area's Lightbox with the plant behind the glass, the real vial render, zumatides.com, QR to https://zumatides.com/shop (decode-checked), "Research, refined."
- `sticker-set-sheet.png`: the whole set. `build_set.py` rebuilds `set.html` (needs ombre.js, logo-defs.svg, qr.json and the hero-vial renders beside it).
