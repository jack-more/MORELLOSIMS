# ZUMA sticker pack (throw-ins)

Nine die-cut stickers to drop into orders. Files in `print/` are transparent PNGs at 600 dpi with a 2 mm white border built in: upload them as they are to a die-cut sticker printer (e.g. Sticker Mule, StickerApp) and choose "die cut", **no extra border**, since it is already in the art. Sizes below are the artwork; each file adds the border.

| File | Sticker | Size |
|---|---|---|
| zuma-sticker-crest-seal.png | Crest seal, rim line "ZUMA · Research, refined · Est. 2026" | Ø 60 mm |
| zuma-sticker-lightbox.png | Lightbox grid in Oxblood tones, crest + wordmark | 70 × 50 mm |
| zuma-sticker-vial.png | Vial silhouette, Oxblood cap | 28 × 70 mm |
| zuma-sticker-new-era.png | "The new era of research" strip | 100 × 24 mm |
| zuma-sticker-six-areas.png | Six research-area dots, "Six areas · one standard" | 86 × 30 mm |
| zuma-sticker-lot-stamp.png | "Every lot tested" postage stamp (cut follows the perforated edge) | 50 × 62 mm |
| zuma-sticker-zuma-beach.png | Zuma Beach sun and sea, coordinates | 62 × 62 mm |
| zuma-sticker-lab-label.png | Write-on lab label: sample, date, initials (matte so pens work) | 70 × 40 mm |
| zuma-sticker-wordmark.png | Plain ZUMA wordmark | 80 × 19 mm |

Material: matte white vinyl for all; the lab label must be matte (writable). Suggested first run: 250 of each, or 500 of the three you like most.
Source: `build_stickers.py` (designs) → `stickers.html`; `node export.cjs` re-exports `print/`.
