# ZUMA sticker set · Post Spanish (direction A)

Fourteen die-cut stickers, no border: the cut follows the art. Seven rectangles, 70 × 100 mm: one per research area (Metabolic · RT-3, Dermal · GHK-Cu, Endocrine · Tesamorelin, Tissue · BPC+TB, Neuro · Selank, Lab supplies · BAC water) and one family sticker with all six Lightboxes.

- `print/zuma-sticker-*.png`: transparent PNG, about 500 dpi. Upload as-is to a die-cut printer (Sticker Mule, StickerApp), "die cut", border OFF. Matte vinyl.
- Every sticker: the area's Lightbox full-bleed with the plant behind the glass and the real vial render across it; the QR is a Snow tile set into the grid (top right) to https://zumatides.com/shop, decode-checked on all seven; the Oxblood label band carries the custom ZUMA wordmark edge to edge in Snow with ZUMATIDES.COM spaced to the same width under it.
- `sticker-set-sheet.png`: the whole set. `build_set.py` rebuilds `set.html` (needs ombre.js, logo-defs.svg, qr.json and the hero-vial renders beside it).

## Circle seals

Seven 60 mm seals (`zuma-sticker-seal-*.png`): the area's Lightbox fills the circle, the crest in Oxblood at the centre, "ZUMA · RESEARCH, REFINED" over the top and "ZUMATIDES.COM" along the bottom, both upright, in Oxblood; the Oxblood seal carries them in Snow. Sheet: `seals-sheet.png`.
