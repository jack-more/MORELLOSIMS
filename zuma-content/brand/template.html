Position

The house for researchers who pay their own way.

Research peptides are sold two ways: cheap with nothing to check, or checkable at a price only a funded lab can pay. ZUMA sits where those meet. Every lot is tested and the report is public, and the price is posted per mg so anyone can compare.

For

Independent researchers and small labs who buy research compounds with their own money.

Who need

Compounds they can verify, at a price they can keep paying.

ZUMA is

A research peptide house: twelve compounds in six colour-coded areas, each lot tested by HPLC and MS before it ships.

Because

Every vial carries its lot number and a QR code to that lot's report, and every product shows its price per mg.

Against

One old COA reused for years, review farms, and markups that only work because buyers can't check.

Buyer one

The independent researcher

Price-sensitive and careful. Wants the report first and the number second. Converts on price per mg and the lot QR.

Buyer two

The repeat lab

Orders the same compounds every month. Converts on standing orders (save 10%, ships every 30 days) and sets (three or more compounds, save 10%).

Buyer three

The skeptic

Knows the field and assumes every seller is lying. Converts when the lot number on the vial matches a report with a date, a method and a lab.

Rules for the line
  • Keep the comma and both periods: “Research, refined.” Then “At prices researchers can afford.”
  • The audience is always “researchers”. Never “everyone”, “you” or “people”. The word keeps the product in the lab and keeps the line honest about who we sell to.
  • Never swap in “affordable”, “cheap” or “budget”. “Can afford” reads as fair. The others read as low quality.
  • No puns or remixes (“refined prices”, “researched, refined”). One line, said the same way every time, is what builds recall.
  • The price line never sits next to a person, a body or a use. It sits next to a vial or a number.

Voice

Write like the lab tech who's always right.

Short, plain and exact. US English, sentence case, no exclamation marks, no emoji, no urgency. The confidence comes from the numbers, so give the numbers.

Exact.

Name the method, the unit and the lot. “HPLC · MS”, “10 mg”, “−20 °C”. A space before every unit, a true minus sign, the middle dot as separator.

Plain.

Compound names as printed on the label. Headings say what the section is. Buttons are verbs: “Add to cart · $99”, “Build a set”, “Open the report”.

Unhurried.

No countdowns, no “selling fast”, no all-caps. A lot sells out, it says “Sold out · next lot in testing”, and that's the whole story.

Price rules
  • Show price per mg on every product sold by weight, under the price, in mono.
  • The number on the page is the number you pay. Shipping is stated before checkout.
  • Cross out a price only if we charged it for a real stretch of time. The current “launch” compare-at prices were never charged, so they come off when the new prices go on.
  • Two discounts and only two: Set, three or more compounds, save 10%. Standing order, ships every 30 days, save 10%. Same names on the site, cart, emails and posts.
  • In a headline, state the number. Never “affordable”, “cheap” or “low prices”.

Rules

The lines we don't cross.

These keep the store, the payment rails and the accounts alive. They apply to the site, the feed, DMs, packaging and anything a partner posts for us.

On every product surface

For laboratory research use only. Not for human or veterinary use.

Once per surface, where it applies. Vial, carton, product page, cart, product posts.

Describe by mechanism

Say what the molecule is and what it binds, then “Studied in … research models.” Never what it does in a person or an animal.

No use talk, anywhere

No amounts, schedules, combinations or routes, in posts, replies or DMs. Use the reply template and stop.

Reviews and claims

No paid, swapped or written-for-us reviews. Don't repost a customer's claim about use, even a happy one.

$ZUMA and referrals

A utility, a membership or a loyalty program. Never an investment, a return or a price prediction.

Pictures

Product only. No people, hands, bodies, syringes, pills, or lab coats on models.
