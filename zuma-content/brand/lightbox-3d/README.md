# ZUMA Lightbox, live 3D (10 Nov 2026)

The Lightbox as real-time 3D in the browser, after the founders' reference (three.js wired privacy glass with plants behind it).
Hammered privacy glass with a fine wire mesh and the Lightbox tile joints, palm and bamboo leaves swaying behind it,
refracted and frosted through the glass, and a pool of light that follows the pointer or finger. One research colour at a time
(Tissue, Metabolic, Dermal, Endocrine, Neuro, ZUMA grey), never blended.

- `index.html`: the whole thing, plain WebGL in one file (no libraries). `?area=metabolic` picks the colour; `?t=3` freezes a frame for stills.
- `lightbox-live.mp4`: 8 seconds of it moving. `lightbox-3d-tissue-metabolic.jpg`: stills.
- Not on the live site; it deploys only when a founder says "deploy".
