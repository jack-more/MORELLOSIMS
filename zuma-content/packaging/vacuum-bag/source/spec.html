ZUMA vacuum bag · print spec

Custom printed vacuum bag, three-side seal · 7 versions, one size · front and back printed · for quotation and sampling

The bag

TypeVacuum bag, three-side seal. Top edge open: we fill and vacuum-seal it ourselves.
Size70 × 105 mm finished (W × H). Side and bottom seals 5 mm. Keep the top 12 mm free of seal.
ContentsOne vial per bag: 3 mL (Ø 16 × 35 mm), or 10 mL (Ø 24 × 64 mm) for BAC water and NAD+. Please confirm 70 × 105 mm takes the 10 mL vial and still seals.
FilmFood-grade vacuum film (PA/PE or PET/PE), suitable for a chamber vacuum sealer. Please recommend thickness.
PrintFull coverage, both sides, reverse printed. White underlay under all ink (the bag is fully printed, not clear). CMYK + Oxblood spot colour #6E1C2A (please propose the Pantone match).
FinishMatte preferred. Please quote matte and gloss.
Bottom sealThe research-use line sits 5 mm from the bottom edge: please keep the bottom seal smooth (no knurl) or tell us and we will move the line up.
QR codeOn the back, must scan: links to https://zumatides.com/lots. Please scan the proof before printing.
