BACKDROP=<image> BDW/BDH (cm) BDZ=2.0 BDSTR=1.1, plus the usual PW=10 PH=15 CLENS=70 ... VIALS=NN@1.1, 720 x 960, 40 samples.
Over white the film reads the print plate's sky blue; over a scene, the scene shows through the blue and the palm prints on the back.
