ZUMA vacuum bag: print spec (blue)

One design. 10,000 bags. 4 plates.

The bag

TypeFlat vacuum pouch, sealed on 3 sides, open at the top. We fill it with two glass vials and vacuum seal the top ourselves.
Size100 x 150 mm outside. 5 mm seals. Tear notch on both sides, 18 mm from the top. Square corners.
MaterialPET/PA/PE laminate, clear and glossy. Please confirm the thickness you use for vacuum packing small glass vials.

The 4 plates

1. Front: blueTransparent blue ink in tints (the light and dark blue squares), printed on clear film with no white behind it. It must stay see-through like tinted glass: the vials inside must show through the blue. File: vector/ZUMA-bag-front-blue.ai
2. Front: whiteOpaque white on the logo and text only. The blue is knocked out under it. File: vector/ZUMA-bag-front-white-ink-plate.ai (black in this file = white ink).
3. Back: whiteOpaque white over the whole back panel.
4. Back: blackThe leaf shadow in black ink, up to 72%, printed on the white. File (plates 3 and 4): vector/ZUMA-bag-back.ai

Files

Vector (.ai)Type converted to outlines. Page 106 x 156 mm (the 100 x 150 mm bag plus 3 mm bleed), with the cut, seals and notches drawn as thin lines (do not print them). Print runs over the seals to the edge.
