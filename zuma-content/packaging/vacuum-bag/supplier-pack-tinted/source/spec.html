ZUMA vacuum bag: print spec

Three front designs (red, blue, grey) and one back design shared by all three.

The bag

TypeFlat vacuum pouch, sealed on 3 sides, open at the top. We fill it with two glass vials and vacuum seal the top ourselves.
Size100 x 150 mm outside. 5 mm seals. Tear notch on both sides, 18 mm from the top. Square corners (tell us if round corners are possible at no extra cost).
MaterialPET/PA/PE laminate, clear and glossy, as in your listing. Please recommend the thickness for vacuum packing two small glass vials (we expect about 90 to 100 microns total).

Front panel (3 designs)

Plate 1: colourTransparent ink, printed on clear film with no white behind it. It must stay see-through like tinted glass: the vials inside must be visible through the colour. One file per design: front-red, front-blue, front-grey.
Plate 2: whiteOpaque white, only on the logo and text. The colour plate is knocked out under it. The same file for all three designs.

Back panel (same for all 3)

PrintOpaque white over the whole back panel, then the leaf shadow in grey (black ink, up to 72%) on top.

Files (folder "print")

PagesEvery file is 106 x 156 mm: the 100 x 150 mm bag plus 3 mm bleed. 600 dpi. Print runs over the seals to the edge.
