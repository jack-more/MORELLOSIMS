# Shipping: silicone capsule + bubble mailer

No box. Each vial ships in its silicone capsule (the protection), the capsules go in a stock burgundy poly bubble mailer, and one round crest seal closes the flap.

- **Seal:** `zuma-seal-2in-oxblood.pdf` (Snow on Oxblood; for light mailers) or `zuma-seal-2in-snow.pdf` (Oxblood on Snow; for burgundy mailers). 2 in (50.8 mm) circle, the PDF page is 54.8 mm with 2 mm bleed: order as a 2" round die-cut sticker, matte, permanent adhesive.
- **Mailer:** stock poly bubble mailer, burgundy or wine. #000 (4 × 8 in) for 1–2 capsules, #0 (6 × 10 in) for 3–6.
- **Orders of 3+:** add a thin card stiffener so the mailer cannot fold.
- **Card:** the lot card (QR to the lot report, research-use line) goes in with the capsules.
- The capsule spec (Rev B) now requires a 3.0 mm wall and a drop and crush test, since nothing else protects the glass.
