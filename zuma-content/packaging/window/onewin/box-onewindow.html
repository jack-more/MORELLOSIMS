ZUMA TIDES · PACKAGING · WINDOW CARTON · ONE-WINDOW VERSION

One window, printed panels

Same panel layout as the hero render. Only one small window is cut, in the middle of the tall centre panel: through it you see the crest on the vial label and, above the label, the coloured inside of the box in shadow through the glass. Every other panel is printed solid area colour on the Snow board. The whole inside of the sheet is flood-printed in the area colour. One sheet, no sleeve, no liner.

01 · FRONT PANEL, ABOUT 6:1 · THREE PANEL TEXTURES

Green dashed = the one die-cut window (open)Colour = printed panel (not cut)Snow = soft-touch board
A · Raised rib emboss 击凸细横纹 0.6 mm
Fine horizontal ribs, 0.6 mm pitch, blind-embossed into the printed panels. Reads as tide lines and feels ridged under the thumb.
B · Gloss spot-UV on soft-touch 局部UV（触感膜底）
Box is soft-touch matte; only the colour panels get gloss UV, so they catch light like lit glass and feel slick against the velvet board.
C · Raised linen emboss 布纹压纹
A linen-weave texture embossed into the colour panels only. Warm and tactile, the most "paper" of the three.
