ZUMA Tides · Window carton · Rev F · insert 内卡 · draft for approval

The insert: a coloured cradle

One die-cut card folds into a U (back + two sides) with a shelf on top. The vial drops through a Ø16.5 mm hole in the shelf and stands on the box floor, centred and label-forward in the window. The shelf sits just above the label, behind solid board, so nothing of the insert shows in the window. Open the top and the vial is nested in solid area colour. The carton inside goes back to plain white.

Construction choice: a U-cradle with a fold-forward shelf. One piece, no glue, nothing in front of the window; the shelf rests on the side walls and its up-turned lip braces against the carton front, so it can't sag. Shelf height 23.0 mm (not 24): the vial body is straight only to 23.5 mm, so the hole grips the body, not the shoulder.
