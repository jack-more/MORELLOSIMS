# Points programs, crypto referral/affiliate systems, social-token failures and sybil resistance (for ZUMA program design)

Research date: 17 October 2026. Figures are dated where the source dates them. "Prior state" means a figure or rule that may since have changed. Most coverage of 2023-2024 programs is press and on-chain dashboards cited by press. Primary docs are used where available (Hyperliquid, Jupiter, Civic, Solana Foundation, Human Passport, Gitcoin).

ZUMA context assumed throughout: a small Solana research-peptide shop. $ZUMA holders get member pricing. Referrals pay 10% of referred orders' net value times a holdings-based level multiplier, capped at 30%. Members vote on which factory makes each lot and earn a voting streak worth +2% discount and +0.1x referral per consecutive round, up to +6% / +0.3x.

## 1. Points programs (Blur, Blast, EigenLayer, Hyperliquid, Kamino, Jupiter, Ethena): design, retention after airdrop, backlash, best practices

### Takeaway
Points reliably buy fast deposits and volume, but most of it leaves once rewards stop. The worst cases were Blast (TVL down more than 60% from peak within weeks of the June 2024 drop) and Friend.tech. The backlash usually comes from expectations set by opaque points that are then disappointed: EigenLayer (non-transferable token, geoblocking, linear allocation) and Kamino (linear allocation, last-minute snapshot). Hyperliquid is the main counter-example: no VCs, 31% of supply to about 94k users, and activity kept growing after the drop. Its points rewarded real fee-paying trading on a product that already had product-market fit.

### Cited Findings
**Blur (NFT marketplace; Season 1 airdrop Feb 2023, Season 2 ended 21 Nov 2023)**
- Season 2 rewards used "loyalty": listing exclusively on Blur earned 100% loyalty, and Blur said it discouraged gaming such as unrealistic prices or listing dead collections. The Season 2 pool was 300M BLUR, and one wallet claimed about 22.85M BLUR (about $11M at the time). — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/blur-token-rewards-nft-marketplace-airdrop); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/121326/nft-traders-flex-blur-airdrops-most-tokens)
- Wash trading: CryptoSlam flagged at least $577M of Blur-linked NFT sales as wash trades. These were resales within short windows at near-identical prices, which it read as self-trading to farm points. — [Forkast](https://forkast.news/at-least-us577-million-blur-linked-nft-sales-wash-trades-cryptoslam-says/)
- Delphi Digital's counterpoint: only about 11% of Blur volume was flagged as wash trading. Delphi argued that Blur rewarded bids and listings (liquidity) rather than raw volume, which reduced the incentive to wash trade. — [Delphi Digital](https://members.delphidigital.io/feed/is-blurs-volume-legit)
- With zero marketplace fees and zero royalties, nothing but gas discouraged point farming. — [nftnow](https://nftnow.com/features/is-blur-backing-itself-into-a-corner/)
- Galaxy Research: Blur reached about 80% of NFT volume after the Feb 2023 airdrop, with OpenSea at about 15.5%, and much of this was traders positioning for Season 2. Blockworks called the 2023 surge "inorganic". — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/blur-airdrop-nft-trading)
- Delphi warned that the volume dominance was incentive-driven and likely to fade once incentives ended. — [Delphi Digital](https://members.delphidigital.io/feed/blur-after-the-airdrop)

**Blast (L2; Phase 1 airdrop 26 June 2024)**
- Phase 1 gave 7% of supply to Blast Points, 7% to Blast Gold and 3% to Blur stakers. Half of the 100B BLAST supply is earmarked for the community. Sources disagree on Phase 2 length (4 vs 12 months). — [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/long-awaited-blast-token-drops-at-usd420m-market-cap); [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/blast-ethereum-rollup-hacked)
- TVL fell from an all-time high of $2.3B (5 June 2024) to about $875M after the airdrop, a drop of more than 60% (DefiLlama via The Defiant). Blockworks reported chain TVL roughly halved and activity down about 35% from the June peak. — [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/blast-struggles-after-its-highly-anticipated-airdrop); [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/blast-ethereum-rollup-hacked)
- A later non-primary piece claims Blast DAU fell from more than 160k to under 4k (about 95%). It is unverified against on-chain data. — [CryptoTimes](https://www.cryptotimes.io/insights/the-2-8-billion-ghost-town-inside-blast-networks-95-collapse/)

**EigenLayer (EIGEN "stakedrop", spring 2024)**
- Complaints: EIGEN was claimable but non-transferable with no date set; users in about 30 countries, including the US, Canada and China, were geoblocked even though deposits had been accepted from them; Pendle and other liquid restaking protocols were excluded. Only 15% of the 1.67B supply was earmarked for the community. — [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/research-and-opinion/eigenlayer-airdrop-outcry-shows-how-points-are-raising-trader-expectations); [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/eigen-airdrop-elicits-pushback-from-eigenlayer-community)
- Linear-by-points allocation: by EigenLayer's own estimate, the top 2% of stakers would receive about 90% of the drop and the bottom 36% about 0.2%. The Foundation said linear allocation was chosen for sybil resistance. After the backlash it raised the minimum to 110 EIGEN per Season 1 user, and on 2 May 2024 added 100 EIGEN for pre-29 April users. — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/eigenlayer-airdrop-additional-eigen-token-days-after-backlash); [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/research-and-opinion/eigenlayer-airdrop-outcry-shows-how-points-are-raising-trader-expectations)

- CoinDesk framed the EIGEN episode as possibly signalling "the demise of points". — [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2024/05/09/eigenlayers-eigen-airdrop-might-signal-demise-of-once-popular-points)

Peak value is cited as about $10.8B (ForkLog) or about $12B (Ryder), which are commentary figures. — [ForkLog](https://forklog.com/en/hype-or-a-new-standard-what-hyperliquids-airdrop-historys-most-generous-teaches-us/); [Ryder](https://ryder.id/blogs/post/hyperliquid-hype-one-year-after-the-12b-airdrop); [OneKey](https://onekey.so/blog/ecosystem/hype-airdrop-breakdown/)

**Kamino (KMNO, snapshot 31 Mar 2024, airdrop Apr 2024)**
- 7% of supply went to an "initial community distribution". The community criticized the linear distribution, which gave no early-adopter boost, and a snapshot date seen as rewarding farm-and-dump. A Kamino admin defended linear allocation as removing the incentive to sybil-farm across thousands of wallets. — [CoinDesk](https://coindesk-coindesk-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/business/2024/03/07/solana-defi-protocol-kamino-sets-kmno-token-airdrop-for-april/); [Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/?p=22161)
- KMNO reportedly fell from $0.11 to $0.04 within ten minutes of debut. This is a secondary report. — [Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/?p=22161)

**Ethena (Shards, Feb to Apr 2024; Sats Season 2)**
- Shards were 5% of the 15B supply (750M ENA). Activity multipliers ran 5x to 20x shards per day. Anyone who unstaked, unlocked or sold all their USDe before 1 April was ineligible. The top 2,000 wallets got 50% linear vesting over 6 months. — [ForkLog](https://forklog.com/en/ethena-labs-to-airdrop-750-million-ena-tokens/); [DefiPrime](https://defiprime.com/airdrop/Ethena)
- ENA launched 2 April 2024 at about $0.59 and a market cap of about $1B. The Sats campaign added BTC collateral and was to end on 2 Sept 2024 or earlier if USDe TVL hit a target. Sources disagree on whether the target was $5B or $10B. — [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/ethena-labs-ena-launches-at-usd1-billion-post-airdrop-as-sats-campaign-kicks-off); [DL News](https://dlnews.com/articles/defi/ethena-500m-airdrop-leads-defi-project-to-plan-the-next-one/)

**Jupiter (JUP; Jupuary 2025; Active Staking Rewards)**
- A community overview says Jupuary 2025 sent 700M JUP to about 2M wallets, including "Super Voters" who voted on 13 or more of 17 proposals. Its anti-sybil measures included on-chain and fee-paying analysis, a Jupiverse profile to aggregate wallets, and excluding low-value bot swaps. This is a forum post, not an official figure. — [Jupiter forum](https://discuss.jup.ag/t/jupiter-jup-airdrop-balanced-proposal-for-jupuary-2025/26573?page=4)
- ASR is covered in Section 6.

**Industry trend (2025-2026)**
- Qualitative reporting says 2026 airdrops are fewer, more selective and tied to real product usage. The norm is weighting by fees paid rather than wallet count, with conversion formulas never fully disclosed. These are secondary and partly promotional sources. — [2theMoon Substack](https://2themoon.substack.com/p/why-airdrops-are-becoming-more-selective); [LBank](https://www.lbank.com/creator/airdrops-2026-dead)
- A guide recommends measuring retention at 3, 6 and 12 months after TGE, and says points work best layered on genuine product-market fit. — [DefiPrime](https://defiprime.com/points-based-token-distribution-programs-web3)

### Inferences
- The programs that held up (Hyperliquid, and Ethena's "hold until snapshot" rule) rewarded behaviour that is valuable even without the reward: fee-paying trades, held deposits. Programs that rewarded cheap actions were farmed: Blur listings with no fees, Blast deposits. ZUMA's equivalent of "fee-paying" is a paid, delivered and not-refunded order. Rewards keyed to net order value, not clicks, sign-ups or votes, start on the right side of this line.
- Backlash came from undisclosed formulas followed by a disappointing conversion. ZUMA's rewards are fixed formulas (10% x multiplier, +2% per streak round), which avoids the "points are an IOU" problem. Changing the formula later would recreate it, so rule changes should be announced ahead of time and never applied retroactively.
- Linear-by-stake allocation is sybil-neutral because splitting wallets gains nothing, but it is whale-concentrating (EigenLayer's top 2% got about 90%). ZUMA's 30% cap on the referral rate is the right kind of guard against concentration.

### Gaps
- I found no rigorous cohort-level retention study (for example D30/D90 of airdrop recipients) for Blur, Blast, Kamino or Ethena. Only TVL and volume snapshots are available.
- I found no 2024-2026 Blur market-share data after the points ended.
- I found no primary-source final outcome of Ethena's Sats campaign, and no official Jupuary 2025 numbers.

## 2. Social/creator tokens (Friend.tech, Farcaster, Lens, DeSo): rise, collapse, lessons

### Takeaway
"Strong-finance" social tokens collapsed once speculation stopped. In these designs each person's key or coin is a tradable bonding-curve asset (Friend.tech, BitClout/DeSo). Friend.tech made about $90M in fees and then handed its contracts to a null address in Sept 2024. Farcaster grew without a token, using Warps credits, USDC creator rewards and a wallet, but stalled. It was sold to Neynar in Jan 2026 while returning $180M to investors. The lesson the industry drew was "weak finance, strong social".

### Cited Findings
**Friend.tech (Base; launched Aug 2023)**
- Daily fees peaked near $1.7M on 21 Aug 2023 and fell more than 87% to about $215k by 26 Aug. Transactions fell more than 90% from a peak of about 525k. — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/friendtech-pronounced-dead-inflows-activity-declines)
- V2 and the FRIEND airdrop (May 2024): users could claim 10% after following 10 users and the other 90% only after joining a Club. The launch was buggy. FRIEND wicked to $169 and then traded below $1.30, and fell 46% ($2.78 to $1.49) within 4 hours as farmers sold. — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/friend-tech-v2-launch-non-transferable-token-concerns); [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/markets/friend-tech-token-tanks-after-shambled-debut); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/230170/friend-tech-price-bounces-back-23-after-chaotic-v2-launch?amp=1)
- Shutdown (reported 9 Sept 2024): admin and ownership were transferred to a null address. Lifetime fees were about $90M, of which about $44M went to the team (21.co data). Deposits fell 92% from $52M in Oct 2023 to $4M. Daily new users were in single digits, daily fees under $100 (from about $2M), and FRIEND was down 98% since May. At its peak Friend.tech had been more than half of all Base activity. — [DL News](https://www.dlnews.com/articles/defi/friend-tech-shuts-down-after-revenue-and-users-plummet/)
- Coverage noted controversy over the business model and alleged bot speculation. — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com//cryptocurrency/from-crypto-darling-to-debacle-in-two-weeks-friendtechs-revenue-drops-by-70/)

**Farcaster (no protocol token)**
- Prior state (to Jan 2026): no official token. "Warps" were an off-chain credit managed by Warpcast. A creator-rewards program paid about $25k USDC per week to top posters, and a $10/month "Pro" tier was planned, with revenue to flow into creator rewards. — [Bankless](https://www.bankless.com/es/read/whats-new-with-farcaster); [The Block](https://theblock.co/post/302665/farcaster-is-getting-in-app-usdc-payments)
- The community token DEGEN became Farcaster's "unofficial token" alongside Warps. — [Dune](https://dune.com/blog/farcaster-and-lens)
- DAU estimates (one outlet): a peak of about 73.7k to 100k in July 2024, falling to about 40k to 60k by Oct 2025, with only about 4,360 "genuinely engaged" Power Badge daily users. These are single-outlet estimates. — [BlockEden](https://blockeden.xyz/blog/2025/10/28/farcaster-in-2025-the-protocol-paradox/)
- Neynar retrained its user-quality score in May 2025 to catch spam networks and LLM slop. — [Neynar](https://neynar.com/blog/retraining-neynar-user-score-algorithm)
- On 21 Jan 2026 Neynar acquired the Farcaster protocol, app and Clanker. Merkle Manufactory planned to return the full $180M raised to investors, and the founders stepped back. Romero said "Farcaster is not shutting down", with about 250k MAU in Dec 2025 and more than 100k funded wallets. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/386549/haun-backed-neynar-acquires-farcaster-after-founders-pivot-to-wallet-app); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/386816/farcaster-repays-venture-backers); [Neynar](https://neynar.com/blog/neynar-is-acquiring-farcaster)

**Lens / DeSo**
- After Mask took over Lens stewardship (around Jan 2026), Mask's founder called Friend.tech's "strong finance" model wrong and argued for "weak finance, strong social". Vitalik criticized social projects leaning on tokens and hype. The source is a KuCoin Ventures report, so it is weak. — [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/ae-kucoin-ventures-weekly-report-what-happened-to-web3-social-unicorns-when-gold-is-more-trusted-than-the-dollar-a-global-crisis-of-confidence)
- BitClout/DeSo preloaded about 15k Twitter profiles with creator coins without consent, which drew backlash. The founder later called it a prototype he regretted. The coin fell about 30% on the founder's wire-fraud charge in July 2024. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BitClout); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/11/16/lessons-from-a-crypto-entrepreneur-a-conversation-with-nader-al-jani); [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2024/07/31/charted-your-token-when-your-founder-is-busted-crypto)

### Inferences
- Friend.tech's revenue was speculation on the asset, not use of a service. Once price momentum ended, nothing held users. ZUMA's token utility is a discount on goods people actually buy, which is "weak finance, strong product". Marketing should keep it that way rather than selling $ZUMA as a price-appreciation play.
- Gating claims behind actions (Friend.tech's "join a club" for 90%) produced farm-and-dump, not engagement. Requiring a vote to unlock a discount has the same shape, and Section 6 discusses this.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable primary DAU/MAU series for Farcaster after the 2026 acquisition, and no data on Warps economics.
- I found no current data on Lens Protocol usage.

## 3. Referral/affiliate programs in crypto (Hyperliquid, Binance/Bybit, Pump.fun, Jupiter, Blinks): abuse and countermeasures; holding-based multipliers and pitfalls

### Takeaway
Well-designed crypto referral programs share four traits: modest rates (Hyperliquid pays referrers 10% of fees), hard caps on how long or how much a referral pays (the first $1B of a referee's volume; the referee discount only on the first $25M), an earn-in to become a referrer ($10k volume), and one immutable binding per referee (Binance). Holding- or staking-linked tiers are common (Binance's 2019 BNB tiers, HYPE staking fee discounts and the proposed staking-referral tiers). Their main pitfalls are whale capture, borrow-to-boost and snapshot gaming. Self-referral is countered by identity/KYC, device, IP and payment overlap checks, and withholding rewards on cancelled or high-risk orders.

### Cited Findings
**Hyperliquid (docs, read Oct 2026)**
- Any account with $10,000 in volume can create a code. The referee gets a 4% fee discount on their first $25M of volume. The referrer gets 10% of the referee's fees (less the discount) on the referee's first $1B of volume. Rewards are claimable above $1. Vaults and sub-accounts are excluded from discounts. — [Hyperliquid Docs: Referrals](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/referrals)
- Builder codes: a per-order fee the builder keeps, capped at 0.1% on perps and 1% on spot. The user must approve a maximum fee with their main wallet, and can hold at most 10 active approvals. The builder needs at least 100 USDC perps account value. Builder codes take precedence over referral codes on an order, and referral codes are disabled after a user trades $1B cumulative. — [Hyperliquid Docs: Builder codes](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/builder-codes)
- Proposed staking referral program: referrers and builders who stake HYPE keep up to 40% of referred users' fees at the top staking tier, and can share up to 50% back to the referee. The kept share falls as the referee's VIP tier rises (100% at VIP0 to 40% at VIP6). The docs state it was not yet live on testnet or mainnet when cached, so its current status is unconfirmed. — [Hyperliquid Docs: Staking referral program](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/referrals/staking-referral-program)
- Holding-based discount analog: the HYPE staking fee discount runs from 5% (>10 HYPE) through 10%, 15%, 20% and 30% to 40% (>500k HYPE). Staking links are permanent and cannot be unlinked. — [Hyperliquid Docs: Fees](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/fees)
- Builder-code outcomes: CoinGecko ranked Phantom first among builders at about $20.63M cumulative revenue, with Based about $15.06M and MetaMask about $6.51M. A later update put Phantom above $25M. Phantom charges 0.05%. Program-wide totals conflict across sources ($23M to $50M) because of different dates and definitions. — [CryptoPotato/CoinGecko](https://cryptopotato.com/hyperliquid-builder-program-becomes-major-revenue-engine-for-wallets-and-bots-coingecko/); [Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/news/phantom-tops-hyperliquids-builder-program-with-206m-in-fees)

**Binance / Bybit**
- Binance Wallet referral: commission tiers run from 30% to 80% based on referees' 30-day rolling volume (top tier at $2.5M or more). Tiers drop only after 10 consecutive days below threshold. Each referee can bind only one code and cannot switch referrers. — [Binance Wallet Referral FAQ](https://www.binance.com/learn/binance-wallet-referral-program)
- Prior state (2019): the Binance Futures referral base rate was 20% or 30% depending on BNB holdings, with the referrer able to share 10% with invitees. This is a holding-linked tier, and the program may no longer apply. — [Binance (2019)](https://www.binance.com/en/support/articles/360037520232)
- Bybit: third-party pages cite affiliate tiers of 30%, 40% and 50% by referral volume. These conflict with other directories (top 45%) and could not be confirmed from Bybit terms. Bybit's TradFi referral program (Oct 2025) pays 20%, 25% or 30% for 1, 5 or 100 qualified invitees. — [TradersUnion](https://tradersunion.com/brokers/crypto/view/bybit/bybit-affiliate-program/); [PR Newswire](https://tools.prnewswire.com/en-us/live/20813/release/20251024EN06222)

**Pump.fun creator fees**
- Prior state (May 2025): a flat 0.05% creator fee. Dynamic Fees V1 (Sept 2025) set creator fees from 0.95% for market caps under $300k down to 0.05% above $20M. Co-founder Alon Cohen said it doubled bonding-curve volume but created "a dangerous imbalance", encouraging low-risk token creation over trading. — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/pumpdotfun-fee-model); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/337872/pump-funs-new-fee-model-hands-out-2m-to-creators-in-first-24-hours)
- Jan 2026 revision: creator fees can be split across up to 10 wallets, ownership transferred, and percentages set after launch (including by community-takeover admins). The team plans a "market-based approach" in which traders decide which narratives deserve creator fees. — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/pumpfun-revamps-creator-fees-fee-sharing-cto-controls)

**Jupiter referral / Solana Blinks**
- The Jupiter Referral Program is an open-source on-chain program. Projects set `default_share_bps` for referrers, and referrers create referral accounts plus per-mint token accounts. Since Jan 2025, Swap API integrators can pass any token account as `feeAccount`, while the Trigger API still uses the Referral Program. One docs page says Jupiter takes 2.5% of integrator platform fees. — [Jupiter Developers](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/tool-kits/referral-program); [Jupiter Hub](https://hub.jup.ag/docs/swap-api/add-fees-to-swap)
- Solana Actions/Blinks (launched June 2024) turn an Action, an HTTP endpoint that returns a signable transaction, into a shareable URL. Phantom and Solflare are the main clients. No adoption or referral-commerce metrics were found. — [Solana Foundation](https://solana.com/it/news/solana-actions-blinks-simplify-transactions-onchain); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/301693/solana-foundation-targets-turning-any-website-or-app-into-starting-point-for-crypto-transactions)

**Abuse patterns and countermeasures**
- Observed at a crypto lender: one person verifying ID on one device and completing on a phone in another city; multiple accounts using the same ID; and multiple accounts withdrawing referral rewards to the same wallet, up to 30 times. The main defense was requiring identity verification to be eligible for referral rewards. — [HackerNoon (Lendingblock)](https://sia.hackernoon.com/the-way-of-the-scammer-what-to-look-out-for-when-running-a-crypto-referral-scheme)
- E-commerce controls (vendor sources): cookie and device fingerprinting; IP matching to flag self-referrals and rings; payment-method overlap detection; email match between affiliate and customer; allow, flag or reject modes, with Reject recommended; automatically withholding rewards on cancelled or high-risk orders; velocity limits (3 to 5 per day, 10 to 20 per month); and mapping the referral graph to find closed loops. — [AffiliateWP](https://affiliatewp.com/docs/anti-fraud/); [Affonso](https://affonso.io/help/risk-center/self-referral-detection); [Track360](https://www.track360.io/learn/refer-a-friend-program-design/preventing-referral-fraud); [Growave](https://www.growave.io/blogs/growth-and-retention/how-to-prevent-abuse-in-referral-programs-for-e-commerce-growth)
- Holding-based weight pitfalls: Beanstalk (17 Apr 2022) was drained via a flash loan because voting power was read at execution time rather than from a past snapshot. Snapshot-at-prior-block (the OpenZeppelin Governor default) neutralizes borrowed tokens. An NYU arXiv paper (2025) proposes time-weighted snapshots. Loss figures conflict ($77M vs $182M). — [Beanstalk disclosures](https://docs.bean.money/almanac/disclosures); [Veridise](https://veridise.com/?p=20343); [arXiv 2505.00888](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00888?context=cs)
- Linear-by-stake systems concentrate rewards: EigenLayer's own estimate was that the top 2% received about 90% (see Section 1). In Jupiter's ASR thread, a commenter cited about 90% of DAO voting power sitting with about 1% of wallets. — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/eigenlayer-airdrop-additional-eigen-token-days-after-backlash); [Jupiter forum](https://discuss.jup.ag/t/asr-active-staking-rewards-methodology/19338)

### Inferences (ZUMA-specific)
- ZUMA's referral can reach 30% plus a 0.3x streak boost. That is generous next to Hyperliquid (10% of fees), but ZUMA pays on order value rather than fees, so it is a direct margin cost. This makes self-referral (buying through your own code for a 10-30% rebate) the primary abuse vector. It amounts to an unadvertised extra discount for holders. Countermeasures that fit a shop:
  - Bind each customer to one referrer once, permanently (Binance rule).
  - Pay out only after the order is delivered and past the refund/chargeback window, and claw back on refund.
  - Block payouts when the referrer and referee share a shipping address, payment wallet, funding wallet cluster, device, IP or email domain.
  - Require referrers to have their own paid order history (Hyperliquid-style $10k earn-in, scaled down).
  - Cap per-referee lifetime payout (Hyperliquid caps at the first $1B), for example the first N orders or first $X.
- Read the holdings multiplier from a time-weighted average balance, or require a minimum holding/lock period, not a spot balance at order time. Otherwise someone can buy or borrow $ZUMA right before a big referred order and sell after. Linked staking or lock with an unbonding delay is the pattern Hyperliquid uses (permanent staking links, staking tiers).
- Holding-tied rates resemble securities-style "return on holding" more than a plain discount. Keeping the multiplier as a perk on referred commerce, not on passive holding, is the safer framing. This is an inference, not legal advice.

### Gaps
- I found no official Binance or Bybit text on self-referral enforcement or clawbacks, and could not verify Bybit's current tiers from primary sources.
- I found no data on Pump.fun creator-fee wash trading, and no Blinks referral-commerce metrics.
- The live status of Hyperliquid's staking referral program as of Oct 2026 is unconfirmed.

## 4. Brand/commerce loyalty tokens (Starbucks Odyssey, Nike .Swoosh/RTFKT, Reddit, Shopify token-gating, Coinbase One): why they failed or worked

### Takeaway
Brand Web3 loyalty mostly failed when the token was the product: tradable collectible "stamps", PFPs or speculative points that depended on the NFT market. They held up best where the chain was hidden and the benefit was a plain commerce perk: Reddit's card-purchased "Collectible Avatars" with custodial Vaults, Shopify's gated discounts, and Coinbase One's fee-free subscription. Reddit's tradable Community Points (Moons) were ended in Oct 2023, citing scale and regulation, and crashed more than 80%, amid insider-selling allegations.

### Cited Findings
- **Starbucks Odyssey:** launched late 2022 with NFT "Stamps" earned through Journeys on Polygon/Nifty Gateway. The beta closed on 31 March 2024, with Journeys due by 25 March; points expired at the end of March, and stamps were kept and tradable. The statement mentioned refocusing and giving no future details. Fading NFT interest and technical friction for mainstream users were the reasons most often cited. — [NRN](https://www.nrn.com/quick-service/starbucks-shutters-odyssey-its-nft-backed-metaverse-program); [nftnow](https://nftnow.com/news/starbucks-odyssey-nft-rewards-beta-to-close-at-end-of-month/); [The Spoon](https://thespoon.tech/not-surprisingly-starbucks-is-shutting-down-its-nft-program/)
- A blog claims Odyssey ran about 18 months with about $200k in sales. This is unverified. — [BlockEden](https://blockeden.xyz/blog/2026/03/14/traditional-brands-web3-pivot-nike-starbucks-porsche-nft-failure-loyalty-utility/)
- **Nike RTFKT:** the wind-down was announced in Dec 2024 and completed by the end of January 2025, citing falling Clone X prices and NFT disinterest. .Swoosh was not shut but launched no new collections. An April 2025 class action alleges Nike sold unregistered securities (more than $5M in damages claimed). — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/294410/nike-killing-nft-sneaker-rtfkt); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/amp/post/329097/nike-owned-nft-wearables-startup-rtfkt-is-winding-down); [BlockEden](https://blockeden.xyz/blog/2026/03/14/traditional-brands-web3-pivot-nike-starbucks-porsche-nft-failure-loyalty-utility/)
- **Reddit Collectible Avatars (2022):** more than 3M Vault wallets were created, about 2.5M of them to buy avatars. Avatars were bought with cards, minted on Polygon, and branded without the words "NFT" or "crypto". Early sales were about $2M in 24 hours and more than $6.5M cumulative per Dune at the time. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2427464); [Artnet](https://news.artnet.com/market/reddit-launched-its-own-nft-marketplace-in-july-it-already-has-more-users-than-opensea-and-other-major-platforms-2199244)
- **Reddit Community Points (Moons):** ended on 17 Oct 2023 (phased out by 8 Nov), citing scale and regulation. MOON fell from $0.25 to under $0.04 within hours. On-chain data suggested mods sold before the announcement; mods said they got one hour's notice. Reddit later renounced the contract and burned more than 30% of supply. — [Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/markets/cryptocurrency/23/10/35303061/reddit-is-phasing-out-crypto-powered-community-points-reward-tokens-moon-plunges-over-80); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/amp/post/258476/pair-of-reddit-mods-appear-to-have-sold-off-tokens-ahead-of-announcement-to-terminate-blockchain-based-program)
- **Shopify token-gating:** apps verify NFT or token ownership to unlock products and discounts. Examples include Doodles (holder-only online merch after Art Basel) and Superplastic x Gucci. Shopify says it works best for tech-savvy, highly engaged communities. No public conversion or revenue-lift data was found. — [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/blog/token-gating); [Shopify Doodles](https://en.shopify.nl/retail/doodles)

- **Coinbase One:** a $29.99/month subscription with zero Coinbase trading fees (spread still applies), priority support, account-takeover protection and boosted staking. The press read it as a response to fee pressure. ### Inferences
- The surviving pattern is "membership = discount on things you already buy", with low friction. ZUMA's member pricing fits this pattern. A tradable token adds two exposures. The Reddit Moons episode shows the reputational and legal risk of tradable reward tokens (insider-selling optics, regulatory exit). The RTFKT suit shows securities-claim risk when value is marketed as appreciation.
- Starbucks' lesson: gamified "journeys" plus a separate marketplace added friction for little benefit. ZUMA's vote-and-streak mechanic should be one click inside checkout or the account page, not a separate quest system.

## 5. Sybil resistance: Human Passport, World ID, Civic, Solana Attestation Service, proof of purchase, stake caps, quadratic plus identity; airdrop-hunter detection (Hop, Arbitrum, Optimism, LayerZero); options for a shop

### Takeaway
No single tool gives one person, one reward. Production systems layer cheap identity signals (Passport stamps, Civic CAPTCHA/liveness/uniqueness, World ID tiers) with behavioural and graph clustering (Arbitrum, Optimism, Trusta, Gitcoin COCM). They keep filters secret, and either redistribute sybil allocations (Optimism) or offer amnesty (LayerZero's 15% self-report). Crowdsourced bounty hunting works in moderation (Hop) but floods and is gamed at scale (LayerZero paused it within a day). A shop has a stronger primitive than any of these: paid, shipped orders tied to a delivery address and payment method, which can be attested on-chain via the Solana Attestation Service.

### Cited Findings
**Identity / personhood tools**
- Human Passport (formerly Gitcoin Passport): the score is the sum of stamp weights, and the recommended threshold is 20. The Jan 2026 reweight sets ETH Activity at 22.541, NFT at 22.053, Government ID at 16.026, Gitcoin at 6.237, GitHub at 6.026, ENS at 0.208 and Safe at 0.222. Partners set their own threshold, and the docs warn that not all sybils will be caught at 20. Weights are adjusted as sybil tactics evolve. — [Human Passport stamp weights](https://support.passport.human.tech/stamps/stamp-weights); [Passport docs: thresholds](https://docs.passport.human.tech/building-with-passport/stamps/major-concepts/scoring-thresholds)
- Gitcoin COCM (Connection-Oriented Cluster Matching) dampens QF matching when many donors share donation patterns. Gitcoin says complete sybil resistance is impossible from donation data alone, recommends pairing COCM with Passport's model-based detection, and claims COCM improved allocations by 50% over more than 20 rounds ($5.5M). These are self-reported. — [Gitcoin blog](https://gitcoin.co/blog/leveling-the-field-how-connection-oriented-cluster-matching-strengthens-quadratic-funding); [Gitcoin governance GG20](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/our-sybil-resistance-strategy-for-gg20/18524)
- World ID: three tiers (selfie, government ID, in-person Orb iris scan), and each relying app chooses the level. About 25M verified by Feb 2025 per one profile, and about 33M World App users by Sept 2025. Integrations were announced in April 2026 (Shopify, Zoom, DocuSign, Tinder, Okta), and the Orb Mini ships in 2026. No current Orb-verified count was found. — [Axios](https://axios.com/2026/04/17/worldcoin-zoom-shopify-retail-partnership); [Yespress profile](https://yespress.io/world.md)
- Civic Pass (Solana, via gatekeeper networks), per Civic's docs:
  - CAPTCHA pass: expires after 30 days.
  - Liveness pass: video selfie, refused if a VPN is detected.
  - Uniqueness pass: the face map is compared to encrypted maps of users on the same network to enforce one user, one wallet.
  - ID Verification pass: government ID.
  - Apps query on-chain pass state by network key. — [Civic docs](https://docs.civic.com/pass/use-cases/communities-and-daos/sybil-resistant-id-verification); [Civic Solana integration](https://docs.civic.com/pass/integration-guide/solana)
- Solana Attestation Service (SAS): live on mainnet since 23 May 2025. It is permissionless. Trusted issuers tie off-chain data, such as KYC results, to a wallet without putting personal data on-chain. Its parts are credentials (issuer plus authorized signers), schemas and attestations. Listed uses include KYC passports, regional access control, sybil resistance and accreditation. — [Solana Foundation](https://solana.com/news/solana-attestation-service); [Solana launch docs](https://launch.solana.com/docs/attestations)

**Airdrop-hunter detection**
- Hop (2022): recruited community sybil hunters. It later considered rewarding hunters with 25% of the roughly $315k of HOP they saved. Its public cluster repo was used by Optimism and Arbitrum. — [DL News](https://dlnews.com/articles/defi/hop-protocol-mulls-over-airdrop-farming-tipoff-reward)
- Optimism Airdrop #1 (23 May 2022): removed more than 17,000 of about 250k eligible addresses, holding more than 14M OP, and redistributed them pro rata. About 2,100 were community-flagged, about 9,000 flagged for suspicious L1 activity and more than 11,000 for L2 activity. The filters were not published, "teaching sybils how to become undetectable is not in anyone's best interest". Hop's repo helped remove about 2k addresses. — [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/optimism-sybil); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/148417/optimism-cracks-down-on-airdrop-farmers); [Optimism gov forum](https://gov.optimism.io/t/tooling-utilities-nominations-for-rpgf2/4639/76?u=jonas)
- Arbitrum (Mar 2023): points were deducted for short-lived activity and for low balances with little contract interaction. Graph clustering collapsed highly connected subgraphs (shared funding source, similar transactions, clusters of more than 20 addresses) into a single recipient. Hop and Nansen data were used. Thousands of sybils still got through. — [Arbitrum Foundation docs](https://docs.arbitrum.foundation/concepts/sybil-account); [Beosin](https://beosin.com/resources/a-closer-look-at-the-anti-sybil-mechanism-under-the-arbitrum); [DL News](https://dlnews.com/articles/defi/sybil-attackers-loot-airdrops-for-millions-with-fake-wallets/)
- LayerZero (May 2024):
  - Self-report: a 14-day window from 3 May in which self-reported sybils kept 15% of their allocation. More than 2M addresses were initially flagged, refined to 803,093 using LayerZero, Chaos Labs and Nansen.
  - Bounty phase: hunters needed at least 20 addresses and a clear method, and received 10% of the sybil's allocation, first reporter wins. It drew more than 3,000 reports and 30,000 appeals within hours, accused users counter-reported the accusers, and it was paused after one day. A 0.02 ETH bond was floated.
  - Daily messages then fell below 40k, the lowest since March 2023. — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/layerzero-concludes-sybil-self-reporting-phase); [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/layerzero-labs-identifies-800-000-potential-sybil-addresses-in-public-list); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/295274/layerzero-labs-ceo-announces-pause-of-sybil-bounty-hunter-process-after-influx-of-reports); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/295709/layerzeros-daily-messages-hit-lowest-level-in-over-a-year)
- Academic work and tooling: Trusta's two-phase pipeline uses graph mining to find coordinated communities, then behaviour analysis to cut false positives. A 2025 arXiv model reports 0.98 AUC against 0.86 for clustering-based Trusta on its own dataset (author-reported). A 2026 preprint notes that graph methods need token transfers between wallets and proposes behavioural-similarity detection. — [ethresear.ch (Trusta)](https://ethresear.ch/t/trusta-s-ai-and-machine-learning-framework-for-robust-sybil-resistance-in-airdrops/16828); [arXiv 2505.09313](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.09313v1); [arXiv 2607.27370](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.27370)
- Jupiter tied wallets via a Jupiverse profile and excluded low-value bot swaps (forum overview). Forum users noted that wallet-based detection misses one person voting with several wallets. — [Jupiter forum](https://discuss.jup.ag/t/jupiter-jup-airdrop-balanced-proposal-for-jupuary-2025/26573?page=4)

### Inferences (practical options for ZUMA)
- **Tier 0, proof of purchase:** the account is the unit, not the wallet. One account is one delivery identity (name, address, payment instrument or funding wallet). A delivered, non-refunded order is far costlier to fake than a wallet. Use it as the gate for voting eligibility and for referral payouts.
- **Tier 1, KYC-lite attestation:** issue a ZUMA-signed SAS attestation ("verified researcher, account #, first-order date, orders count bucket") to the customer's wallet. Holdings checks and vote weight can then key on an attested wallet. One attestation per account means extra wallets carry no extra voting weight. ZUMA already has a researcher-attestation flow (the store API in this repo), so the SAS credential can be issued from it.
- **Tier 2, optional uniqueness:** Civic Uniqueness/ID pass or World ID for referrers above a payout threshold only. This adds friction just where money flows, as Lendingblock found ID-gated referrals effective.
- **Detection:** periodic clustering on the shop's own data: shared addresses, payment wallets, funding-source wallets, and order timing around streak deadlines. Keep the rules private (Optimism's rationale). Prefer redistribution or forfeiture over public accusation, and avoid public bounty hunting (LayerZero's failure). An amnesty/self-report path is a low-cost option if a large cleanup is ever needed.
- **Weights:** for votes, one attested account equals one vote, or stake-weighted with a hard per-account cap. Quadratic voting is only meaningful when paired with identity, because without identity splitting wallets beats it, which is why Gitcoin pairs COCM with Passport.

### Gaps
- I found no independent audit of Civic Uniqueness false-positive/negative rates, no current World ID Orb count, and no published issuer list for SAS KYC credentials.
- The final outcome of LayerZero's bounty phase (bond adoption, final sybil count after appeals) was not confirmed.

## 6. Specific risk: paying people to vote (vote-for-discount). Does it create low-quality votes, and how to counter it?

### Takeaway
The evidence is suggestive, not conclusive. Paying for participation reliably raises turnout. Jupiter's ASR pays about 50M JUP per quarter for voting, regardless of direction. But commenters and governance literature warn that it recruits reward-motivated rather than informed voters. In vote markets (Votium/Convex), payments end up deciding outcomes. Jupiter itself paused all DAO votes from June 2025 to 2026 after a "breakdown in trust". ZUMA's streak reward is participation-based, so expect click-through voting unless eligibility, information and secrecy are designed in.

### Cited Findings
- Jupiter ASR methodology (5 July 2024): rewards are proportional to staked JUP at the time of voting times the proposals voted on. Each proposal gets an equal share of the pool. Earlier votes earn more because less voting power has participated. The post does not say vote direction affects rewards. The linear design is meant to "prevent sybil". A 50 JUP minimum was proposed to exclude dust wallets. The Q2 2024 pool was 50M JUP, from a 100M JUP bootstrap. — [Jupiter forum: ASR methodology](https://discuss.jup.ag/t/asr-active-staking-rewards-methodology/19338)
- In that thread a commenter said the 50 JUP minimum "may not incentivize informed voting but will get people to participate". Others cited about 90% of DAO voting power held by about 1% of wallets. — [Jupiter forum: ASR methodology](https://discuss.jup.ag/t/asr-active-staking-rewards-methodology/19338)
- On 20 June 2025 Jupiter DAO suspended governance votes until at least January 2026, citing a "breakdown in trust", centered on the team's large token allocation (20% of 10B JUP) being used to vote. ASR continued at 50M JUP per quarter. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/358988/dao-behind-dex-aggregator-jupiter-suspends-governance-votes-until-early-2026-amid-community-concerns); [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/jupiter-dao-pauses-voting-after-backlash-over-team-s-voting-power); [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/jupiter-halts-governance-voting-to-tackle-burnout-and-refocus-on-innovation/)
- Vote markets: an academic study (arXiv 2311.17589) finds that Votium bribe markets largely determine the outcomes of Convex's fortnightly gauge votes, and cautions DAOs considering the model. Weekly Gauge notes the Votium delegation address controlled about 40% of vlCVX voting power. — [arXiv 2311.17589](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.17589); [Weekly Gauge](https://weeklygauge.substack.com/p/weekly-gauge-13-bribes-game-theory)
- A DL Research/DefiLlama report sets out a taxonomy of DAO vote buying: private deals, bribe marketplaces, auctions, vote-selling platforms and prediction markets. — [DefiLlama Research](https://defillama.com/research/report/an-analysis-of-the-role-of-vote-buying-in-dao-governance-report)
- Scholarship cited in a SAGE IS paper notes that voting incentives can signal participation, but can also produce voting "primarily motivated to obtain financial rewards". I reached it only via a proxy, so verify the DOI 10.1177/02683962251343627. — [SAGE (via proxy)](https://https-sage-cnpereading-com-443.webvpn1.xju.edu.cn/doi/10.1177/02683962251343627)
- Outside crypto: lab experiments in the US and Kenya (Leight, Foarta, Pande, Ralston, J. Public Econ. 2020) find that vote payments reduce voters' willingness to hold politicians accountable, more so when payments are broadly targeted. This is a loose analog only. — [Yale EGC](https://egc.yale.edu/pande-value-money-vote-buying-and-politician-accountability)
- Hidden tallies: Snapshot shielded voting (with Shutter, since 2022) threshold-encrypts votes until the proposal closes, so only total voting power cast is visible meanwhile. It aims to reduce strategic late voting and vote-buying. An ENS forum post cites Rossello (2024) documenting "sniping blockholders" across 75 DAOs. Shutter self-reports more than 382 communities and more than 216k shielded votes, undated. — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/105201/snapshot-adds-shielded-voting-daos-help-solve-voter-apathy); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/158084/snapshot-to-let-daos-hide-votes-with-threshold-encryption); [ENS forum](https://discuss.ens.domains/t/temp-check-shielded-voting-for-ens-snapshot-proposals/22142); [Karma/Shutter](https://www.karmahq.xyz/project/shielded-voting/about)
- Snapshot-at-prior-block or time-weighted balances stop borrowed or flash-bought tokens from voting (Beanstalk lesson, Section 3). — [Veridise](https://veridise.com/?p=20343); [arXiv 2505.00888](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00888?context=cs)
- The Friend.tech V2 airdrop required social actions (follow 10, join a club) to claim. Farmers did them and sold immediately: FRIEND fell 46% in 4 hours. — [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/markets/friend-tech-token-tanks-after-shambled-debut)

### Inferences (countermeasures for ZUMA's factory vote)
- **Risk:** the streak gives up to +6% discount and +0.3x referral just for voting every round. The marginal voter will be discount-motivated and will likely pick the default, the first option or the visible leader. A factory choice affects product quality and safety, so low-information votes have a real cost, not just a governance-optics cost.
- **Eligibility:** only accounts with at least one delivered, non-refunded order in the trailing N months, plus a SAS/researcher attestation, can earn streak credit. Holding-only wallets can still vote, but not earn streak perks. This ties the reward to actual customers, one per account.
- **Weight:** one attested account, one vote, or holdings-weighted with a per-account cap. Read holdings from a snapshot taken before the round opens, or a time-weighted average, with a minimum hold/lock period (for example, tokens held or locked at least 14 to 30 days). This blocks buy-vote-sell.
- **Secrecy:** use hidden tallies (commit-reveal or a Shutter-style shielded vote) so voters cannot bandwagon on the leader or be paid for a provable vote. Do not let a voter prove their choice to a third party during the round.
- **Information:** show each factory's COA/HPLC history, lead time and price per lot on the ballot. Consider requiring the voter to open the lot dossier, or answer one comprehension check, before the vote counts toward a streak. This is an inference; it is not tested in sources.
- **Abstain and decay:** allow a counted "abstain/no preference" option so uninformed members are not forced into noise. Have streak perks decay after a missed round rather than reset to zero, to reduce last-minute vote farming. Keep the +6% cap.
- **Separate reward from outcome power:** pay streak credit for participation, but let only verified purchasers' votes decide the factory, or have the team retain a quality veto for any factory failing third-party testing. Disclose team and insider voting power up front; Jupiter's backlash was about team votes.
- **Audit:** watch for vote clusters (same IP, device or address across accounts voting identically at the last minute), which is the same graph logic as Arbitrum and Optimism.

### Gaps
- I found no peer-reviewed study that directly measures decision quality under participation-only voting rewards (ASR-style), as opposed to vote-buying markets. No published data on how Jupiter ASR voters split by direction, or on the share of "click-through" votes.
- I found no evidence on comprehension-check gating in token governance. That recommendation is untested.
- I could not access the Rossello (2024) "sniping blockholders" paper directly.
