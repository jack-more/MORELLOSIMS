# Community ownership and utility tokens in the Solana and DePIN ecosystem: models for $ZUMA

Research date: 17 October 2026. Every figure carries its date. "Prior state" marks a design that has since changed. Many 2026 figures come from aggregators (CoinMarketCap AI pages, KuCoin, exchange blogs), and those sources often disagree. Each conflict is noted where it applies.

## Jupiter (JUP): airdrops, J4J and working groups, Active Staking Rewards, the DAO pause, Litterbox buybacks, sentiment

### Takeaway
Jupiter made the largest attempt on Solana to pay people for governance. It tied staker rewards (ASR, 50M JUP per quarter) to voting, ran huge "Jupuary" airdrops, and funded DAO working groups. In June 2025 the team paused DAO voting, citing "a breakdown in trust" and a "perpetual FUD cycle." Since then Jupiter has moved toward supply discipline: it burned the Litterbox buyback pile in late 2025, and in February 2026 holders voted to postpone the 700M JUP airdrop and pause team vesting for net-zero emissions. Paid voting produced turnout but not good governance. Paying for votes drew farmers, and governance became a channel for price complaints.

### Cited Findings
**Jupuary airdrops**
- Jupuary 2025: the first vote missed a new 70% passing threshold. A revised proposal then passed with 87% support. — [Jupuary 2025 Overview + Checker, discuss.jup.ag](https://discuss.jup.ag/t/jupuary-2025-overview-checker/32013)
- Jupuary 2025 split (Dec 2024 draft): about 425M JUP to users, 75M to stakers and 200M to the "Carrots" and "Good Cats" reward systems. Claims opened 22 Jan 2025 with a 3-month window and a sybil/bot appeal deadline of 27 Jan. — [ForkLog](https://forklog.com/en/jupiter-token-declines-ahead-of-700-million-jup-airdrop/)
- Decrypt reported Jupiter's airdrop as about $1.7B in value (Jan 2025). — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/295635/solana-defi-exchange-jupiter-airdrops-1-7-billion)
- Prior state to current: in February 2026 about 75% of voters approved the "net-zero" option. It postponed the 700M JUP Jupuary 2026 airdrop indefinitely, returned the allocation to the Community Cold Multisig and paused team reserve emissions. JUP fell about 3% on the news. — [CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/top-stories/699b6195302cb822e7265462/); [SolanaFloor](https://solanafloor.com/news/jupiter-weighs-plan-to-drive-jup-token-emissions-to-net-zero)
- Conflict: a CoinMarketCap piece dated 12 Apr 2026 reports, from community and influencer posts, that Jupuary was reshaped to 200M for fee-paying users, 200M for stakers and 300M locked for incentives, and pushed to May 2026. It calls this a "71% cut," but the three numbers add to 700M. Treat this as unverified. — [CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/top-stories/69dafdda3b10291756db57cd/)
- Conflict: a secondary report (MEXC) says many new recipients sold right after the 2025 airdrop, and that the community voted in November 2025 to cut the 2026 Jupuary from 700M to 200M. This is not confirmed by a primary source. — [MEXC news](https://www.mexc.com/news/603504)
- Pine Analytics (Q3 2026) says the February 2026 vote "cancel[led] the 2026 airdrop and pause[d] team vesting." Circulating supply stayed flat at 3.32B JUP through Q3 2026, so net issuance was zero. — [Pine Analytics Q3 2026](https://pineanalytics.substack.com/p/jupiter-quarterly-report-q3-2026)

**Active Staking Rewards (ASR) and the June 2025 pause**
- 19–20 June 2025: Jupiter paused DAO voting until the end of 2025 and promised to return in 2026 "with a fresh approach that unifies, rather than divides." Kash Dhanda: "The current DAO structure isn't working as intended… We see the breakdown in trust… the perpetual FUD cycle that grows with every vote… stuck in a negative feedback loop." CoinDesk compared it to Yuga Labs scrapping the ApeCoin DAO earlier in June 2025. JUP was about $0.405 and down 21.8% over 30 days. — [CoinDesk, 20 Jun 2025](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2025/06/20/solana-dex-jupiter-pauses-dao-votes-citing-breakdown-in-trust); [Kash Dhanda on X](https://x.com/kashdhanda/status/1935736103497351573); [Jupiter on X](https://x.com/JupiterExchange/status/1935967452254412805)
- ASR continued at 50M JUP per quarter during the pause. No new DAO-funded working groups were created, so no extra emissions. The Litterbox Trust was to stay "untouched until 2027." — [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2025/06/20/solana-dex-jupiter-pauses-dao-votes-citing-breakdown-in-trust); [Jup DAO FAQ June 2025](https://discuss.jup.ag/t/jup-dao-faq-june-2025-edition/38967)
- New ASR structure (Q3–Q4 2025): rewards moved from active voters only to all stakers, weighted by stake. Forum figures: about 672M JUP staked, about 532M average votes per proposal in Q2 2025, and the per-JUP reward dropping from about 0.094 to about 0.074 (roughly 21% lower for people who had been voting). Users complained the change was made "behind closed doors." They worried about rewarding dead wallets, lost keys and airdrop hunters (about 100M JUP may sit in inactive wallets). One user said "No vote so no interest to have ASR." Others warned that fake "dummy votes" to keep rewards flowing would turn governance into "a quiz." — [Forum: DAO pause and the new ASR structure](https://discuss.jup.ag/t/dao-pause-and-the-new-asr-structure/38908)
- Ideas the community proposed to stop passive farming: on-chain check-ins, minimum stake held for 2 months (50–100 JUP), weighting by number of votes cast, and weighting by product use. — [same forum thread](https://discuss.jup.ag/t/dao-pause-and-the-new-asr-structure/38908)
- 2026 status: staked JUP rose from 978M to 1,079M in Q3 2026 (about a third of circulating supply). A 50M JUP Q3 staker reward pool opened for claims on 13 Oct 2026. Staking at least 69 JUP is required to trade the new "GUM" perps markets, which ties staking to product access. No governance vote was held July–September 2026. — [Pine Analytics Q3 2026](https://pineanalytics.substack.com/p/jupiter-quarterly-report-q3-2026)
- Working groups and J4J: the forum has an "Introducing Jupiter Working Groups" thread (July 2025) and "J4J #3: Jupuary Vote 2." I could not get primary detail on the specific working-group disputes. — [discuss.jup.ag DAO category](https://discuss.jup.ag/c/jupiter-dao/10)

**Litterbox Trust buybacks and the burn**
- 50% of Jupiter's on-chain revenue goes to the Litterbox Trust, which buys JUP on the open market. In late Oct/Nov 2025, as part of a "Fresh Start" initiative, Jupiter put to a vote burning about 130M JUP (about 4% of circulating supply), with a separate vote on future Litterbox revenue to follow. — [Jupiter on X](https://x.com/JupiterExchange/status/1983973510918893730)
- A secondary source says the burn happened in October 2025 in a package that also cut the unstaking window from 30 to 7 days and removed about 4% of supply ("Burn the Litterbox"). — [search summary of CoinMarketCap/MEXC coverage](https://coinmarketcap.com/top-stories/699b6195302cb822e7265462/)
- Q3 2026: Jupiter revenue was $17.0M (+12% QoQ), of which swap fees were $8.85M and perps $5.6M (−18%). The trust bought 29.5M JUP (about $6.4M), down from 39.7M (about $7.5M) in Q2. Purchases equalled 84% of the trust's revenue share in Q3 versus 114% in Q2. The trust held 143.8M JUP on 30 Jun and 173.3M on 30 Sep 2026 (5.2% of circulating supply), so it accumulated again after the burn. JUP ended Q3 2026 at $0.330 (+52% QoQ) with a $1.10B market cap. — [Pine Analytics Q3 2026](https://pineanalytics.substack.com/p/jupiter-quarterly-report-q3-2026)

### Inferences
- Paying for votes (ASR v1) drew mercenary "click-vote" behavior, and the community itself could not agree whether ASR was a governance wage or a gift. For ZUMA, a voting-streak bonus has the same weakness. Votes that cost nothing and decide little will be farmed. Votes on things that matter (which factory makes a lot) need to be informed, and bad outcomes should cost the voter something.
- Jupiter's arc runs from large airdrops and a DAO with wide scope, through the pause, to a burn, supply discipline and stake-for-product-access. The same sequence shows up across Solana: projects retreat from broad governance toward simple revenue-backed value and product utility.
- Jupiter runs 50% of revenue through buybacks, and ZUMA would do the same at small scale. Market buybacks in a thin market mostly hand money to sellers. Burns and buybacks also do not fix trust when emissions or unlocks keep running alongside them.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm a primary record of the Litterbox burn vote outcome and exact amount, or the final form of Jupuary 2026 (cancelled versus reshaped 200/200/300 in May 2026).
- I found no primary account of what governance "returned" in 2026. Pine reports no votes in Q3 2026.
- I found no primary detail on J4J/working-group budget disputes.

## Pump.fun (PUMP): token sale, revenue, buybacks, reception, criticism

### Takeaway
PUMP raised $500–600M in about 12 minutes in a July 2025 ICO at $0.004. It traded below the ICO price within weeks, and by April 2026 it was about 84% below its all-time high, even though 100% of revenue was going to buybacks. In April 2026 the team burned about $370M of bought-back PUMP (36% of circulating supply) and locked 50% of net revenue into an immutable buyback-and-burn contract for 12 months. The main criticisms: buybacks are small next to turnover and unlocks, holders have no legal claim on revenue, the token has no utility beyond buybacks, and the founders reversed their earlier stance against presales.

### Cited Findings
- ICO (July 2025): $0.004 per token. Raise reported as $500M in 12 minutes ([Blockonomi](https://blockonomi.com/pump-fun-pump-price-ico-raises-500-million-in-12-minutes-at-0-004-per-token/); [Cryptopolitan](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/pump-fun-raises-500-million-in-12-minutes-through-pump-ico/)) or $600M ([Bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/pump-fun-burns-370-million-pump-tokens-revenue-buyback/)). Some reports put the combined public and private raise at about $1B. Sources conflict.
- Backlash at the ICO: critics called it "liquidity extraction" from the memecoin market and accused the team of cashing out after earlier statements against presales. — [Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/pump-fun-draws-backlash-after-confirming-pump-ico/)
- PUMP fell below its $0.004 ICO price. Only about 3% of early investors who had sold or transferred later increased their holdings. — [CoinGape](https://coingape.com/pump-funs-pump-token-falls-below-ico-price/)
- 29 Apr 2026: two on-chain burns totalling about $370M. Lookonchain showed 128.22B PUMP (about $233M) burned that day. Circulating supply fell 36%. New program: 50% of net fees from the bonding curve, PumpSwap and Terminal go to open-market buy-and-burn through a locked contract for 12 months. Prior state: 100% of revenue went to buybacks. Lifetime revenue was over $1B. PUMP rose about 7% on the news, to a $631.7M market cap and $1.9B FDV, about 84% below its July 2025 ATH of $0.01214. — [Bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/pump-fun-burns-370-million-pump-tokens-revenue-buyback/); [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/cryptocurrencies/news/pumpfun-burns-36-of-circulating-pump-supply-unveils-50-revenue-buyback-program-202604290053)
- Cumulative buyback figures conflict: over $300M ([KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/articles/pump-fun-surpasses-300-million-in-pump-token-buybacks-a-deep-dive-into-the-platform-s-deflationary-strategy)), "over $200M in 2026" as of about 1 Oct 2026 ([WalletInvestor](https://walletinvestor.com/news/crypto-news/pump-funs-pump-token-nears-0-006-as-its-buybacks-pass-200-million-in-2026/)), over $466M all-time as of late Sep 2026 ([CoinCentral](https://coincentral.com/pump-fun-pump-price-coin-jumps-20-as-buybacks-pass-466-million)). All are aggregator-grade.
- Revenue pace: 30-day revenue reported at $65.6M (CoinMarketCap AI page, date unclear) and $35.7M in an earlier period when Pump.fun overtook Hyperliquid on 30-day revenue. — [CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/pump-fun/latest-updates/); [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/pump-fun-fees-top-10m-as-revenue-overtakes-hyperliquid/)
- Criticism: buybacks are about 2% of turnover, and team (200B) and investor (130B) tokens unlock from July 2026 ([8blocks](https://8blocks.io/blog/pump-fun-token-buyback)). The July 12, 2026 unlock was about 82.5B PUMP, estimated at about 20% of circulating supply ([CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/top-stories/6a49ade2d7e6c506bb6928a5/); [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/pump-fun-faces-pressure-as-6-875b-pump-unlock-approaches)). The token has no staking, no utility and no fee share, gives holders no legal claim on revenue, and the company faces pending litigation ([The DeFi Report](https://thedefireport.io/research/the-watch-list-pump-fun-pump)).

### Inferences
- Pump.fun shows that a highly profitable business with large buybacks can still have a token that falls 80%+. Buybacks cannot offset a large insider float and unlocks. A token with only a buyback, and no use inside the product, gets priced as a weak equity proxy.
- The founders' reversal on presales hurt trust for a long time. ZUMA's public commitments about supply, insider allocation and lockups need to be few, simple and kept.
- Making the buyback contract immutable for a fixed 12-month term is a useful credibility device (it can't be quietly switched off) and could be scaled down for ZUMA.

### Gaps
- I found no primary Pump.fun disclosure of exact cumulative buybacks. Figures conflict across aggregators.
- The "Curve founder blasts Pump.fun" piece (Aug 2026) was not retrievable. Its content is unknown.

## Jito (JTO) and the Jito DAO fee routing; Kamino, Drift, Marinade and Tensor points, airdrops and governance

### Takeaway
Jito is the clearest case on Solana of a DAO that controls real cash flow. Through TipRouter and JIP-24 (Aug 2025), fees from MEV tips and priority fees go to the DAO treasury rather than to Jito Labs. JTO still works as governance rather than a direct claim on cash. Points-to-airdrop seasons (Kamino, Drift, Tensor, Parcl) reliably caused a usage spike followed by a cliff. Jito was the outlier, because its points rewarded a product (JitoSOL) that was useful in its own right. Marinade shows the common Solana governance stack: token-locked voting on Realms plus a small Council multisig.

### Cited Findings
- JTO airdrop: snapshot 25 Nov 2023. Eligible were wallets with at least 100 Jito Points and searchers who tipped at least 1 SOL cumulatively. Claims were open for 18 months and closed in June 2025. — [Jito blog](https://www.jito.network/blog/jto-airdrop-eligibility-and-allocation-specifications/); [airdrops.io](https://airdrops.io/jito/)
- After the airdrop (data to mid-2024), Jito deposits beat withdrawals in most weeks, with over 10k users depositing each week. Market share rose from 43.5% to 46.2%, and JitoSOL-staked SOL went from 6.76M to 11.05M (+63%). Tiger/Delphi called this an "extreme outlier." — [Tiger Research / Delphi, Aug 2024](https://reports.tiger-research.com/p/do-airdrops-hurt-more-than-they-help-eng)
- TipRouter: originally a flat 3% fee on distributed tips, with about 2.7% going to the DAO. After an upgrade it took 3% on tips plus 1.5% on priority fees, split 90% to the DAO, 5% to LST vault operators and 5% to JTO vault operators. — [What is Jito TipRouter](https://www.jito.network/blog/what-is-jito-tiprouter/); [TipRouter upgrade](https://www.jito.network/blog/tiprouter-upgrade-facilitating-priority-fees/)
- JIP-24 (passed Aug 2025): the Block Engine's 6% MEV fee goes entirely to the DAO treasury. Prior state: split 3%/3% with Jito Labs. JTO is described as primarily a governance token. Treasury uses (buybacks, fee-switch vaults, yield subsidies) are decided by a Cryptoeconomics SubDAO. — [tokenomics.com](https://tokenomics.com/articles/jito-tokenomics-how-jto-captures-mev-and-staking-revenue-on-solana); [DefiLlama Jito DAO](https://defillama.com/protocol/jito-dao)
- Kamino: the airdrop was linear in Season 1 points, which an admin said removes the payoff from splitting activity across thousands of wallets. About 190k of 390k interacting wallets made fewer than 5 transactions, a likely sign of sybil/farmer activity. The snapshot announcement caused controversy. — [Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/solana-defi-protocol-kaminos-announcement-of-airdrop-snapshot-sparks-controversy/); [Tiger Research](https://reports.tiger-research.com/p/do-airdrops-hurt-more-than-they-help-eng); [CoinDesk Mar 2024](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2024/03/07/solana-defi-protocol-kamino-sets-kmno-token-airdrop-for-april)
- Kamino post-airdrop (mid-2024): lending TVL grew by $725M, but more wallets withdrew than deposited. DAU fell to about 3k, of whom 98% were returning users. Small users left once their allocations proved tiny. — [Tiger Research](https://reports.tiger-research.com/p/do-airdrops-hurt-more-than-they-help-eng)
- Parcl (Solana): TVL fell 74% after the airdrop, from a $185M peak to $47M, and DAU fell from over 10k to about 500. Over 158k wallets interacted fewer than 3 times. — [Tiger Research](https://reports.tiger-research.com/p/do-airdrops-hurt-more-than-they-help-eng)
- Tensor: TNSR supply 1B, airdrop 125M (12.5%) from points seasons for NFT traders. Drift: points aimed at active traders. A Drift representative advised farming only with at least $15–20k in weekly volume. — [Medium/airdrop guide](https://medium.com/coinmonks/5-in-one-solana-airdrop-guide-solblaze-marginfi-kamino-sharky-tensor-6d98505f97ff); [ADVFN](https://uk.advfn.com/newspaper/azeez-mustapha/73471/farming-for-free-are-solana-airdrops-still-worth-it)
- Marinade: MNDE holders lock tokens on Realms (SPL Governance) to vote, with a 30-day unlock. A 7-member Marinade Council runs a 4/7 multisig for parameters and operations. In 2023 program upgrade authority moved from a 13-party multisig (6 signatures needed) to Realms. The signers were elected by 21 contributors via quadratic voting in Dec 2022. Sources conflict on whether the MNDE treasury is still team-multisig controlled. — [Marinade help](https://help.marinade.finance/the-structure-of-marinades-dao); [Marinade docs](https://docs.marinade.finance/governance); [Marinade blog Oct 2023](https://marinade.finance/blog/marinade-governance-update-upgraded-liquid-staking-contract-controlled-by-the-dao); [MNDE tokenomics update](https://marinade.finance/blog/mnde-tokenomics-update-revisiting-incentives/)
- Design lessons from the Delphi/Tiger study: run anti-sybil work, set stricter eligibility, be wary of linear allocation (it skews to whales), plan to retain small users, and track organic signals after the snapshot (new versus returning users, median transaction size, retention). — [Tiger Research](https://reports.tiger-research.com/p/do-airdrops-hurt-more-than-they-help-eng)

### Inferences
- Retention after a reward depends on whether the underlying action is worth doing without the reward. JitoSOL was a better staking product, Parcl's synthetic real-estate perps were not, and the post-airdrop curves follow that. For ZUMA, the member price must stand on its own as a good deal. The token should add to an already useful action (buying), not stand in for one.
- Jito's model (fees to a DAO treasury, decided by a narrow SubDAO) shows how to give holders real but narrow powers. That matches ZUMA's plan to let members vote on which factory makes each lot rather than on everything.
- Marinade's Realms vote plus Council multisig is the standard Solana pattern a small project can copy cheaply: token-weighted signalling plus a multisig that executes.

### Gaps
- I found no primary post-mortems with price data for the Drift (DRIFT) or Tensor (TNSR) airdrops.
- I did not verify current JTO price or DAO treasury size. DefiLlama snapshot figures in search results ($0.54, $9.8M per 30 days) were undated.

## Helium, Hivemapper, Render and other DePIN: contributor rewards, burn-and-mint, proof of real-world work, gaming

### Takeaway
DePIN tokens pay contributors for verifiable real-world work (radio coverage, mapped roads, GPU renders) and tie supply to demand through burn-and-mint: customers burn tokens to buy service, and new tokens go to contributors. Helium's history shows both sides. Location spoofing and fake witness rings took a meaningful share of rewards. That led to denylists, trust scores and eventually token consolidation (HIP-138). By late 2025 to 2026 Helium claims DC burns at or above emissions, driven by carrier offload. Hivemapper ties rewards to coverage, freshness and quality and steers contributors with targeted bounties.

### Cited Findings
**Helium**
- Mechanism: HNT is burned to create Data Credits (DC, a fixed-USD service unit), and new HNT is minted on a fixed schedule to reward contributors. — [Frontiers in Blockchain DePIN review (2025)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/blockchain/articles/10.3389/fbloc.2025.1644115/full)
- Solana migration (Apr 2023): each hotspot is minted as a compressed NFT on Solana. — [Solana case study](https://solana.com/news/case-study-helium-technical-guide)
- HIP-138 (approved late 2024, effective about 29 Jan 2025) consolidated rewards back into HNT and retired the separate IOT and MOBILE subnetwork tokens. Some explainers are out of date on this. — [7blocklabs/Medium summaries](https://medium.com/@connect.hashblock/7-helium-hivemapper-tokenomics-lessons-that-actually-last-5eecf3cd4b89); [MillionMiner guide, May 2026](https://millionminer.com/news/helium-mining-2026-complete-operator-guide)
- Halving in Aug 2025 cut annual HNT issuance from 15M to 7.5M. Since Aug 2025, HNT equal to 100% of Helium Mobile subscriber revenue is burned. — [MillionMiner](https://millionminer.com/news/helium-mining-2026-complete-operator-guide); [BlockEden forum](https://blockeden.xyz/forum/t/heliums-data-credit-burns-doubled-to-361k-month-at-t-integrated-its-network-but-the-zero-plan-now-has-fees-is-depin-telecom-growing-up-or-selling-out/883)
- Helium Q1 2026 investor report: network revenue (sum of DC burn, normalized) was $3.9M, −25.6% QoQ. Carrier offload is now the main burn source. Discretionary DC burn from the Helium Mobile MVNO "effectively ceased." — [Blockworks-hosted Helium IR report Q1 2026 (PDF)](https://blockworks.com/api/investor-report/investor-relations-report-q1-2026/pdf)
- Forum figures (unreconciled, treat with caution): DC burn about $6.2k/day in Q1 2025 and about $30.9k/day in Q3 2025. Q4 2025 burns reportedly exceeded daily emissions (about 2,400 HNT/day). 541k+ Helium Mobile subscribers in Nov 2025, up from about 115k. AT&T Passpoint offload began Apr 2025 across 62k+ hotspots. On 27 Jan 2026 the "$0 Zero plan" began charging $5–8/month in taxes and fees and legacy plans were force-migrated, which early adopters called a betrayal. — [BlockEden forum](https://blockeden.xyz/forum/t/heliums-data-credit-burns-doubled-to-361k-month-at-t-integrated-its-network-but-the-zero-plan-now-has-fees-is-depin-telecom-growing-up-or-selling-out/883); [Blockworks: path to deflation](https://blockworks.com/news/helium-potential-path-deflationary-tokenomics)
- Single-sourced and unconfirmed: Helium sold Helium Mobile to a buyer named "Noble." — [Journal du Coin](https://journalducoin.com/?p=806987)
- Gaming and spoofing: a Medium analysis counted about 25k of 524k hotspots (just under 5%) on the denylist as confirmed fraudulent, the "scammers." The denylist was updated almost daily and had an appeals process. HIP-66 proposed a hotspot "Trust Score" because "spoofers are still thriving" and only a small fraction get denylisted. HIP-40 proposed validator-subscribed denylists. — [Tom Tobback, Medium](https://medium.com/@tomtobback/the-helium-denylist-going-after-the-scammers-maybe-part1-613b0a8af89c); [3roam](https://3roam.com/helium-hotspot-spoofing-and-the-deny-list/); [HIP-66](https://github.com/helium/HIP/blob/main/0066-trust-score-and-denylist-convenience.md); [HIP-40](https://github.com/helium/HIP/blob/master/0040-validator-denylist.md); [RAKwireless on gaming](https://news.rakwireless.com/what-is-helium-network-gaming/)
- New attack surface: HIP-130 data-only hotspots skip Proof-of-Coverage verification. Critics worry rewards can be farmed by generating synthetic traffic. — [BlockEden forum](https://blockeden.xyz/forum/t/heliums-data-credit-burns-doubled-to-361k-month-at-t-integrated-its-network-but-the-zero-plan-now-has-fees-is-depin-telecom-growing-up-or-selling-out/883); [Cassiopeia](https://cassiopeia.hk/the-next-helium-scam-earning-hnt-by-generating-data-traffic-over-low-cost-data-only-hotspots/)

**Hivemapper (HONEY)**
- Burn-and-mint (MIP-15): customers buy Map Credits, and an equal amount of HONEY is burned. 75% stays burned and 25% is re-minted as Map Consumption Rewards for contributors, capped at 500k HONEY per week. MIP-19 raised Map Credit prices from 1 Jan 2025. — [Hivemapper Foundation: Burn & Mint](https://www.hivemapperfoundation.org/burn-and-mint); [Hivemapper docs: What is HONEY](https://docs.hivemapper.com/honey-token/what-is-honey/)
- Proof of work and quality: individual reward factors weight coverage, freshness and quality, so mappers drive roads the map needs rather than repeating easy loops. Rewards are paid weekly (Wednesday). — [Individual Reward Factors](https://docs.hivemapper.com/honey-token/earning-honey/individual-reward-factors); [Reward Types](https://docs.hivemapper.com/honey-token/earning-honey/reward-types/); [Earning HONEY](https://docs.hivemapper.com/honey-token/earning-honey/)
- Targeted bounties: the Foundation added 10M HONEY for underserved "priority roads" through 30 Jun 2026. Legacy HDC/HDC-S dashcam rewards ended 4 Feb 2026, and the third-generation "Bee" device became the reward path (Apr 2026). — [CoinMarketCap AI updates](https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/hivemapper/latest-updates/); [Solana Report, Apr 2026](http://solanareport.com/2026/04/20/how-to-deploy-hivemapper-dashcams-on-solana-for-map-rewards-in-2026)

**Render (RENDER)**
- Burn-and-mint: creators burn RENDER (priced in USD) to pay for jobs. Node operators earn from a declining emission schedule (Year 1: 9,126,804 RENDER; Year 2: 5,905,580). ### Inferences
- The DePIN pattern that carries over: reward verified work weighted by usefulness (coverage, freshness and quality, not raw activity), price the service in USD, and connect burns to real customer spending. ZUMA's equivalent of proof of work is a verified purchase or a verified referred purchase (paid order, not chargebacked, past a holding period). It should not reward clicks or sign-ups.
- Helium shows that any reward with a weak link to real-world proof gets gamed at about 5% or more of participants, and that enforcement by denylist is slow and partial. For ZUMA, referral multipliers by holdings invite self-referral rings. Paying referral rewards only after a delay, with a cap per account and KYC- or attestation-gated eligibility, is the counterpart to Proof-of-Coverage.
- Helium's 2026 plan repricing caused a trust backlash, the same "promise reversal" problem seen at Pump.fun. Loyalty terms should not be quietly worsened.

### Gaps

## Solana tooling relevant to ZUMA: Attestation Service, compressed NFTs, Realms, Squads, token-2022, Solana Pay and Blinks

### Takeaway
Solana now has standard building blocks for each part of ZUMA's design. The Solana Attestation Service (SAS) covers off-chain-verified credentials such as KYC, researcher status and geography. Token-2022 NonTransferable gives soulbound membership or reputation tokens, and transfer hooks enforce rules on each transfer. Compressed NFTs give cheap per-unit records, proven at Helium scale. Realms/SPL Governance plus a Squads multisig handle signalling votes plus execution. Solana Pay and Blinks provide checkout and one-click actions (vote, claim, buy) from links.

### Cited Findings
- SAS went live on mainnet in May 2025, announced by the Solana Foundation with the Solana Identity Group. It is an "open source, permissionless protocol to attest to anything." Examples include KYC checks, geographic eligibility, membership and accreditation. — [solana.com: Introducing SAS](https://solana.com/news/solana-attestation-service); [Solana on X](https://x.com/solana/status/1926008981635354646)
- SAS can optionally tokenize attestations using Token-2022 extensions, including NonTransferable, PermanentDelegate and TokenMetadata. This comes from an auto-generated summary of the repo and should be verified there. — [DeepWiki: solana-attestation-service](https://deepwiki.com/solana-foundation/solana-attestation-service)
- Civic reportedly ended its Civic Pass product line in mid-2025, and existing credentials stayed compatible with SAS (secondary source). — [Solana Compass: Civic](https://solanacompass.com/projects/civic)
- Token-2022 NonTransferable: the base layer rejects any transfer of the token, so it is effectively soulbound. Other extensions include transfer fees and interest-bearing tokens. — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/akeempalmer/understanding-solana-token-2022-transfer-fees-interest-bearing-tokens-non-transferable-58pi); [Chainstack](https://chainstack.com/solana-token-2022-utility-extensions/); [Helius: What are Token Extensions](https://www.helius.dev/blog/what-is-token-2022)

This is my synthesis, not an observed deployment.
- Keeping reputation (soulbound, earned) separate from the tradeable token (bought) is the main tool against mercenary farming. Rewards can scale with earned reputation rather than with capital alone.

## Concrete lessons: what drives sustained participation versus mercenary farming

### Takeaway
Across Jupiter, Pump.fun, Solana points programs and DePIN, participation lasted when (1) the rewarded action was worth doing without the reward, (2) rewards were tied to verified real-world work and quality, (3) supply was disciplined and promises kept, and (4) governance scope was narrow and its outcomes mattered. Farming dominated when rewards paid for cheap, unverified actions such as clicks, votes or wallet counts, or for capital alone.

### Cited Findings
- Paying people to vote drew farmers and dead stake. Jupiter's community proposed check-ins, minimum stake held for a period and weighting by vote count, and warned against "quiz-like" dummy votes. — [Jupiter forum](https://discuss.jup.ag/t/dao-pause-and-the-new-asr-structure/38908)
- Broad DAO scope led to a "breakdown in trust" and a "perpetual FUD cycle," and Jupiter paused votes. ApeCoin's DAO was scrapped the same month. — [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2025/06/20/solana-dex-jupiter-pauses-dao-votes-citing-breakdown-in-trust)
- Points-season usage typically collapsed after the snapshot (Parcl: TVL −74%, DAU about 10k to about 500). Jito, whose product was useful on its own, grew. — [Tiger Research](https://reports.tiger-research.com/p/do-airdrops-hurt-more-than-they-help-eng)
- Linear points allocation limits sybil splitting (Kamino) but skews toward whales. — [Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/solana-defi-protocol-kaminos-announcement-of-airdrop-snapshot-sparks-controversy/); [Tiger Research](https://reports.tiger-research.com/p/do-airdrops-hurt-more-than-they-help-eng)
- Buybacks alone did not hold up price against unlocks and reversed promises (PUMP about 84% below ATH in Apr 2026 despite 100% revenue buybacks). — [Bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/pump-fun-burns-370-million-pump-tokens-revenue-buyback/); [8blocks](https://8blocks.io/blog/pump-fun-token-buyback)
- Quality-weighted, verified-work rewards with targeted bounties (Hivemapper) and fraud denylists or trust scores (Helium) are DePIN's anti-gaming tools, and enforcement is still partial. — [Hivemapper docs](https://docs.hivemapper.com/honey-token/earning-honey/individual-reward-factors); [HIP-66](https://github.com/helium/HIP/blob/main/0066-trust-score-and-denylist-convenience.md)
- Tying stake to product access (69 JUP to trade GUM perps) is Jupiter's 2026 move toward utility. — [Pine Analytics](https://pineanalytics.substack.com/p/jupiter-quarterly-report-q3-2026)

### Inferences (applied to ZUMA's design)
- **Member pricing for holders:** fine as a utility, but tiers that require holding (rather than buying) reward capital. Consider requiring tokens to be held over time or staked with an unlock delay (Marinade 30-day lock, Jupiter cut its unstake window from 30 to 7 days) so people can't buy, take the discount and dump.
- **Referral multipliers by holdings:** this is the highest-risk mechanic for sybil and self-referral rings. Pay only on verified, settled, non-refunded orders from attested distinct buyers (SAS), with a delay and per-referrer caps. Making the multiplier depend on earned reputation, not just holdings, reduces whale advantage.
- **Lot-factory voting with a streak bonus:** the streak bonus is ASR v1 in miniature and will draw click-voters. Mitigations: a non-transferable reputation badge for the streak; small bonuses paid in member perks (discounts or credit), not liquid tokens; votes limited to members who bought in the prior N lots (skin in the game); publish lot-test outcomes per factory so votes can be judged; keep vote scope narrow (factory choice from a vetted shortlist, with a team veto on safety and compliance grounds executed by a Squads multisig).
- **Revenue and buybacks:** if ZUMA does buybacks, a fixed-term on-chain commitment (Pump.fun style) is more credible than discretionary ones. But at small-shop revenue, member perks probably beat buybacks for retention, and buybacks invite price-focused communities and securities-like framing.
- **Supply and promises:** avoid large airdrops. Keep insider allocation small, locked and published. Never retroactively worsen member terms (the Helium Zero-plan and Pump.fun presale backlash).

### Gaps
- There is no rigorous dataset on how a streak bonus affects governance quality. The evidence is anecdotal (Jupiter forum).
- Regulatory treatment of utility tokens tied to discounts or votes, and especially to research peptides, is out of scope here and not researched.
